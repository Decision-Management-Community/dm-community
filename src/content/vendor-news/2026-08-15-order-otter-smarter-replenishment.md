Order Otter is a browser-based replenishment tool that models demand and lead-time uncertainty, simulates candidate orders, and recommends the quantity with the best expected economics while showing service level, stockout risk, and leftover-inventory tradeoffs. It is a Bit Bros LLC product built for operations planners without requiring a data science team or ERP integration.
