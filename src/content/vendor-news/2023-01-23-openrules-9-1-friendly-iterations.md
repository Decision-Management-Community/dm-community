title: "OpenRules 9.1 simplifies iterations over business objects"
vendor: "OpenRules"
date: 2023-01-23
url: "https://openrulesdecisionmanager.com/news/release-notes-9-1-0/"

OpenRules 9.1.0 introduced a friendlier way to express iterations over collections of business objects, reducing patterns that previously required multiple tables to a more intuitive single-table form.
