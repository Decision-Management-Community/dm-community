title: "The Decision Factory framework launches"
vendor: "Bit Bros LLC"
date: 2026-09-06

---

Bit Bros LLC has launched The Decision Factory, a supply chain planning methodology for operations leaders in automotive, defense, pharma, and retail. It argues planning drift comes from measuring only visible errors like overstocking while ignoring the hidden cost of lost demand, and makes the case for pricing both directions of error rather than chasing forecast accuracy alone. A companion novel by the same authors dramatizes the ideas.
