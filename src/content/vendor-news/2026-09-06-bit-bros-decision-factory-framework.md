title: "Bit Bros publishes five-stage Decision Factory framework"
vendor: "Bit Bros LLC"
date: 2026-09-06
url: "https://thedecisionfactory.com/"
---

Bit Bros LLC has published **The Decision Factory**, a practitioner framework for structuring recurring decision systems around five stages: **Sense, Predict, Price, Decide, and Commit**. The design automates the first four stages and makes the human approval or override at Commit an explicit part of the feedback loop.
