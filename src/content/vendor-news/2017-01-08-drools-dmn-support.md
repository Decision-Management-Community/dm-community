---
title: "Drools Now Implements DMN"
vendor: "Red Hat (Drools)"
date: 2017-01-08
url: "http://blog.athico.com/2017/01/dmn-runtime-example-with-drools.html"
---

The open-source Drools rules engine added full run-time support for DMN at compliance level 3 in its
upcoming 7.0 release. As of January 2017 the implementation was feature-complete, with the team
working through remaining nice-to-have capabilities and bug fixes ahead of the release.
