---
title: OpenRules 12.2 integrates Rule Engine with Optimization Solvers
vendor: OpenRules
date: 2026-09-01
url: "https://openrulesdecisionmanager.com/release-notes-12-2-0/"
---

OpenRules Release 12.2.0 comes with a brand-new implementation of Rule Solver and Rule Scheduler. It extends the traditional FEEL-like expression language to incorporate optimization concepts—such as constrained variables, constraints, and objectives—directly into regular decision tables. Now, a human or AI modeler can use intuitive expressions to represent complex constraints using a mix of known and unknown decision variables.
