---
title: "Why Is Symbolic AI Being Overshadowed by Statistical AI?"
date: 2023-01-11
speaker: Bas van der Raadt
recordingUrl: https://youtu.be/au-kNF-dYKc
---

There is so much hype around statistical AI. But why? These AI systems can only reproduce old
facts, produce unreliable outcomes, and are not transparent, with architectures that seem to
increasingly factor out human control. So why is the decision-making industry heading in this
direction — and why hasn't symbolic AI, which doesn't suffer from these disadvantages, turned the
tide by offering a more reliable and transparent alternative? Perhaps symbolic AI systems are
perceived as too hard to implement because they require explicit domain knowledge. Could building
them be made easier — could natural-language specification of domain knowledge be an answer? This
talk addresses these questions with a presentation and demo of an experimental Natural Language
Execution (NLE) platform called Hapsah.
