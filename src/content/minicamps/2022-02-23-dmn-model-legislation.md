---
title: Applying DMN to Model Legislation
date: 2022-02-23
speaker: Vincent van Dijk
affiliation: Pharosius.nl
recordingUrl: https://youtu.be/PhS0zig5eYs
---

Vincent shares his experience applying DMN to build the Digital System for Environment and
Planning Act in the Netherlands. Issues discussed: how can we minimize translations and directly
execute DMN? How do we combine models from different sources to deliver a single conclusion? How
do we implement dynamic questioning, and how does this help end users? How do we deal with the
declarative character of DMN in dialogue with the end user? Is DMN suitable for modeling
legislation, and can models be easily read by legal experts?
