We explore how organizations can move beyond model-centric governance toward decision-centric
governance. Using decision-centric orchestration and BPM+ concepts, we examine a practical
framework for classifying AI participation across a spectrum that includes AI tools, assistants,
performers, agents, and AI orchestrators. We also explore how governance requirements evolve as
autonomy increases and introduce a Human-AI Agency Scale (HAAS) for reasoning about
responsibility, oversight, and decision authority.
