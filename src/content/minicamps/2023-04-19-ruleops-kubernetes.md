---
title: "RuleOps: Rule Engines and Kubernetes DevOps"
date: 2023-04-19
speaker: Luca Molteni
affiliation: Red Hat
---

Rule Engines are very powerful tools to encode custom declarative logic — in this talk we look at
what benefits we can get from applying Drools to the Kubernetes Control Plane. From symbolic AI
and inference to CEP, this might be a good fit to solve common Kubernetes administration problems.
This led to "RuleOps," an exploration space providing a toolkit for cloud engineers and DevOps.
