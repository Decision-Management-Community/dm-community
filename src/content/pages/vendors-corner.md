## Prior Announcements (undated)

*The entries below were carried over from the pre-rebuild site as a summary; exact publish dates weren't preserved, so they're kept here rather than in the dated feed above.*

### 2025

**Aletyx** — Offers a "Decision Control Sandbox on AWS" for rapid decision-intelligence deployment, providing DMN modeling with AI features and one-click publishing. Decision Control is powered by the Aletyx Enterprise Build of Drools. Available via AWS Marketplace.

**OpenRules** — Release 11.1.0 introduces an enhanced "Rule Learner" machine-learning product that generates working decision models from business-problem examples, with "Lenses" and "Credits" example templates. Free installation available with model generation and refinement capabilities.

**RapidGen** — Offers large-scale DMN model testing with Tableau visualization integration. Its Test Data Generator identifies logic flaws and behavioral inconsistencies, supporting millions of test items for robustness measurement, and integrates machine-learning components as needed.

**IBM Decision Intelligence** — Describes a "paradigm shift from static rule engines to AI-native decision making," interpreting natural-language policy descriptions and automatically synthesizing business rules with AI-native deployment.

### 2024 and Earlier

Additional vendors featured in past entries include Sparkling Logic, Sapiens Decision, IBM Automation Decision Services, InRule, Trisotech, FlexRule, and Kogito, among others, with various decision-automation and optimization announcements.
