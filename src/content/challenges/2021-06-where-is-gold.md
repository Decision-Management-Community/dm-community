There are three boxes, but only one of them has gold inside. Each box has a message printed on it — exactly one of the three messages is true, and the other two are lies.

- The first box says: "Gold is in this box."
- The second box says: "Gold is not in this box."
- The third box says: "Gold is not in Box 1."

Which box contains the gold?
