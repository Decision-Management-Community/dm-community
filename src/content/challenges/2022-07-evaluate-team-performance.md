Two teams — the Mustangs (Brown, Robinson, Smith) and the Eagles (Black, White, Green) — each played 3 games over 3 days. Each player's efficiency in each game was rated as one of: worst, bad, good, better, or best. Points per game: best = +5, better = +3, good = +2, bad = -2, worst = -5.

| Team | Player | 4/1/2022 | 4/2/2022 | 4/3/2022 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mustangs | Brown | good | better | best |
| Mustangs | Robinson | worst | better | best |
| Mustangs | Smith | bad | good | bad |
| Eagles | Black | good | better | best |
| Eagles | White | worst | better | best |
| Eagles | Green | bad | good | worst |

Build a decision model that totals each player's points across all games and determines which team scored the most overall.
