A company sells Standard and Premium products manufactured in different countries, using a mixed bracket volume-and-cost pricing policy for orders with multiple units:

| Units | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| 1–99 | $10/unit | $15/unit |
| 100–499 | $9/unit | $14/unit |
| 500+ | $8/unit | $13/unit |

For products originating outside the US or Canada, add a $50 handling fee per order. If the order includes hazardous material, increase the total price by 30%.

Example order: 200 Premium units from Germany with hazardous materials; 50 Standard units from the US without hazardous materials; 200 Premium units from Canada without hazardous materials; 150 Standard units from China with hazardous materials.
