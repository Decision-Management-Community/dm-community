A classic small optimization problem, contributed by Alex Fleischer: 300 kids need to travel to the London Zoo. The school can rent 40-seat buses for £500 each and 30-seat buses for £400 each. How many buses of each size should be rented to move all 300 kids while minimizing total cost?
