"Miss Manners" was one of the most popular benchmarks for rule engines twenty years ago, and it's interesting to see how modern decision engines handle it today. Miss Manners is throwing a party and wants to seat her guests boy-girl-boy-girl so that each guest has someone to their left or right with a common hobby. Solve the seating arrangement for parties of 16, 32, 64, and 128 guests.
