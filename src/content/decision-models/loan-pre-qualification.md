This use case was initially published by [KPI](http://kpiusa.com/) and then implemented by
[OpenRules](http://openrules.com).

**Implementation files (Excel format):**

- [Decision.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/LoanPreQualification/main/Decision.xls) — a business decision with a Decision Requirements Diagram (DRD) and links to all other Excel files
- [Glossary.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/LoanPreQualification/include/Glossary.xls) — a business glossary
- [Data.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/LoanPreQualification/include/Data.xls) — test data
- [Rules.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/LoanPreQualification/include/Rules.xls) — decision tables

**Execution results:**

- [results.txt](http://openrules.com/xls/LoanPreQualification/results.txt) — decision execution protocol
- [report.htm](http://openrules.com/xls/LoanPreQualification/report/Report.DetermineLoanPreQualification.html) — decision execution report

You can also view the [ProcessOn diagram with links to the OpenRules decision tables](http://www.processon.com/embed/52bcf2650cf2b8f9adc9c518).
