The [Decision Model and Notation (DMN)](https://www.omg.org/spec/DMN/) standard is developed by the
Object Management Group (OMG). Section 10 of the DMN 1.0 specification describes a decision model
for the Loan Origination process. The executable implementation of this model below was
contributed by [OpenRules](http://openrules.com/dmn_primer.htm).

**Implementation files (Excel format):**

- [Process.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Process.xls) — a business process in the BPMN format with Decision Requirements Diagrams (DRD) in the DMN format
- [Glossary.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Glossary.xls) — a business glossary
- [Data.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Data.xls) — test cases

**Decisions and Decision Tables:**

- [BureauRules.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Decisions/BureauRules.xls)
- [AffordabilityRules.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Decisions/AffordabilityRules.xls)
- [ApplicationScoreRules.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Decisions/ApplicationScoreRules.xls)
- [EligibilityRules.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Decisions/EligibilityRules.xls)
- [RoutingRules.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Decisions/RoutingRules.xls)
- [ReviewRules.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Decisions/ReviewRules.xls)
- [DeclineAcceptRules.xls](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/Decisions/DeclineAcceptRules.xls)

**Execution results:**

- [results.txt](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/result.txt) — decision execution protocol
- [report.htm](http://openrules.com/xls/DMN10/report/run1/Report.EmulateLoanOriginationProcess.html) — decision execution report
