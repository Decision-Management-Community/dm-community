Nine major uses of Agentic AI in decisioning:

1. **Automated Decision Execution** — executing decisions autonomously without human intervention.
2. **Orchestrating Decision Services** — coordinating multiple specialized decision tools dynamically.
3. **Complex Multi-Step Decision Workflows** — handling sequential/parallel decision steps.
4. **Real-Time Adaptive Decisioning** — adjusting decisions based on changing conditions.
5. **Decision Explanation and Audit** — documenting reasoning for transparency.
6. **Human-in-the-Loop Escalation** — recognizing confidence limits and escalating appropriately.
7. **Knowledge Gathering for Decisions** — retrieving and synthesizing information autonomously.
8. **Cross-Domain Decision Coordination** — aligning decisions across departments.
9. **Continuous Decision Improvement** — monitoring outcomes and triggering updates.
