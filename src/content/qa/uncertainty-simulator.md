Dr. Tim Varelmann's pitch for an "uncertainty simulator": formalize alternative policies
mathematically, test each across hundreds of realistic scenarios, tune parameters until the
tradeoffs feel right, then inspect the results visually as a team.

When was the last time your team actually stress-tested a decision against hundreds of possible
futures before committing to it — and what did you use to do it?
