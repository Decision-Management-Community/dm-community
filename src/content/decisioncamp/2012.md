IntelliFest was the 2012 edition of this conference series, bridging RulesFest (2009-2011) and
DecisionCAMP (2013-present). No presentation archive survives for this edition, but the event flyer
and two contemporary write-ups do:

- [Event flyer](https://dmcommunity.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/intellifest2012-flyer.pdf)
- [Blog post by Carole-Ann Matignon, Sparkling Logic](https://techondec.wordpress.com/2012/10/24/intellifest-2012/)
- [Blog post by Carlos Serrano, Sparkling Logic](http://www2.sparklinglogic.com/dmblog/2012/10/24/intellifest-2012-stephen-grossberg-mind-brain-and-autonomous-artificial-intelligence/)

**Organizers:** Jason Morris, Charles Young, Brian Jones, James Owen, Carole-Ann Matignon, Mark
Proctor, Jacob Feldman, Carlos Serrano-Morales, Wolfgang Laun, Andrew Waterman, Brenda Ropoulos,
Fred McClimans, Chandra Mouleeswaran.

There was no DecisionCAMP/RulesFest/IntelliFest edition in 2015 — the series resumed in 2016.
