Building practical decision-making applications requires more than developing decision models. Equally important is the decision harness—the interface that allows end users to interact with decision services, specify additional constraints and preferences, and evaluate results.

This article compares the development of a complete worker scheduling application in 2020 and 2026, using the same decision optimization problem.
