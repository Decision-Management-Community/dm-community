It is interesting that if the DMN standard was dominating the <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/decisioncamp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">last 3</a> DecisionCAMPs, this year many potential presenters warn me that they plan to go far beyond DMN. It seems this year the major discussion scheme will be &#8220;<strong>DMN and Beyond</strong>&#8220;. So, I want to invite all our readers to submit their abstracts or at least to attend DecisionCAMP in Bolzano, Italy on Sep. 17-19. It&#8217;s going to be fun!<!--more--></p>
