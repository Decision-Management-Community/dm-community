<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Decision models (similarly to databases) frequently deal with analysis of collections of objects. This month&#8217;s <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge-march-2024/">Challenge</a> deals with such a situation: you need to help an HR office create a rules-based service to analyze its employees. Each employee&nbsp; has a unique name,&nbsp; age, gender, marital status, locations (places of residence), number of children, salary, and more attributes. This information is coming to the service as a JSON request such as in this&nbsp;<a href="https://github.com/DMCommunity/dmcommunity_shared/blob/master/Employees.json">file</a>. Your service should find answers to questions such as:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>What is an average salary? What are the maximal and minimal salaries?</li>



<li>How many employees are single?</li>



<li>In which states do the employee have residences?</li>



<li>How many people are inside 20% of the highest-paid  employees? List these high-paid employees.</li>
</ul>
