<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Gartner defines the full life cycle application programming interface (API) management market as the market for software that supports all stages of an API’s life cycle — planning and design, implementation and testing, deployment and operation, and versioning and retirement. APIs provide a basis for digital transformation and enable organizations to build business ecosystems, but are challenging to manage and govern. This Magic Quadrant assesses 17 vendors in the fast-evolving full life cycle API management market to help software engineering leaders pick the right one <a href="https://www.gartner.com/doc/reprints?id=1-2BKV56BK&amp;ct=221102&amp;st=sb" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></p>
