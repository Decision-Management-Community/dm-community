<p class="wp-block-paragraph" id="d1e2629"><a href="https://www.kuleuven.be/english/kuleuven" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">KU Leuven</a> scientists published the paper &#8220;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417422017043" target="_blank">Extracting Decision Model and Notation models from text using deep learning techniques</a>&#8220;. It is the first attempt to use deep learning to extract DMN models from text. They classify sentences describing logic or dependencies, then they extract decision dependencies from sentences; and finally labeled and tagged dataset is made available for decision model extraction. It would be interesting to see how this technique is applied to a real-world use case. <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417422017043" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></p>
