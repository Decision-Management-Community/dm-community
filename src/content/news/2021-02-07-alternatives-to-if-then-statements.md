It actually points once again to the <em>difference in how programmers and non-programmers</em> would represent the same business logic. You can find a good example in this old <a href="https://openrules.wordpress.com/2012/01/02/different-decision-tables-for-a-very-simple-use-case" target="_blank" rel="noopener">post</a> that discussed different implementations of a very simple logical statement: &#8220;<em>If x equals to 1, make it 2.
