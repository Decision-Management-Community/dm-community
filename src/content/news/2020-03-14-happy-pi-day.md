Pi Day is observed on <a title="March 14" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_14" target="_blank" rel="noopener">March 14</a> (3/14 in the <i>month/day</i> <a class="mw-redirect" title="Date format" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_format" target="_blank" rel="noopener">format</a>) since 3, 1, and 4 are the first three significant digits of <span class="texhtml mvar">π</span>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"></sup> In 2009, the <a title="United States House of Representatives" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_House_of_Representatives">United States House of Representatives</a> supported the designation of Pi Day.
