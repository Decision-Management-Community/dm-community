<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You need to help a manufacturer decide how much of each demanded product should be produced internally and how much should be sourced from outside. Whether a product is made inside or outside, it has an associated cost. A product can consume a given amount of internal resources that have limited capacities. The general objective is to minimize the total production cost while ensuring the company meets the demand exactly or with a certain tolerance. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The manufacturer wants to consider various production decisions to choose the most suitable ones based on the long-term resource availability and uncertain future demand. <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-dec-2025/">Link</a></p>
