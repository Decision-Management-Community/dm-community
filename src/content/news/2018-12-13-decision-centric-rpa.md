<p>Arash Aghlara <a href="http://www.flexrule.com/archives/decision-centric-rpa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">compares</a> traditional Robotic Process Automation (RPA) with decision-centric RPA when both robots and staff share the logic for repetitive operational business decisions. &#8220;<em>Enabling robots to make such decisions is not about technologies like AI, Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Predictive Analytics, etc. at all. Operational business decisions are a routine part of day-to-day jobs. Staff make decisions day in and day out while performing tasks and activities related to their jobs. Decision-Centric RPA enables robots to make decisions and follow business rules without introducing technical debt to organisations.</em>&#8221; <a href="http://www.flexrule.com/archives/decision-centric-rpa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
