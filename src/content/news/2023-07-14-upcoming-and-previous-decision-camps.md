<p class="wp-block-paragraph">DecisionCAMP is the most popular annual event for Decision Management practitioners. It started in 2008 as October RulesFest, continued in 2009-2011 as RulesFest, became IntelliFest in 2012, and DecisionCAMP since 2013. See the entire <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/decisioncamp/decisioncamp-history/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">history</a> with various links to presentations, blog notes, etc. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This year <a href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">DecisionCAMP</a> will be held online on Sep 18-20. It is interesting to refresh the information from the latest camps and compare it with what to expect in <a href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/program/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">2023</a>:</p>
