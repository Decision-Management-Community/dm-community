Pierre Berlandier ran a candid, side-by-side experimental comparison of rule-based and LLM-based
approaches to the same decision scenario — determining eligibility for a fictitious loyalty program —
and published the results. It's a rare instance of someone actually testing the "rules vs. LLM" debate
rather than just arguing about it in the abstract.
