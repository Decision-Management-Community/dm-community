<p>&#8220;Mirages &amp; Miracles is an augmented reality exhibition where the focus is not on the technology but on the experience. Mirages &amp; miracles is a series of installations in which one is immersed in a very poetic way in virtual, three-dimensional worlds. Augmented drawings, holographic illusions, virtual reality headset are part of this exhibition.&#8221; <a href="https://ars.electronica.art/center/en/exhibitions/miragesmiracles/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
