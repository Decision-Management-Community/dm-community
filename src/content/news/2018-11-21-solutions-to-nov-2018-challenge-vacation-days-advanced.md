Contrary to the <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-jan-2016/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">2016 challenge</a> &#8220;Vacation Days&#8221;, this one doesn&#8217;t have inter-rules relationships like &#8220;apply this rule only if you didn&#8217;t apply this rule&#8221;. It rather provides a constraint across all rules (&#8220;The total number of vacation days cannot exceed 29&#8221;) and asks a decision engine to maximize the total number of given vacation days. Please compare the provided <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-nov-2018/#Solutions" target="_blank" rel="noopener">solutions</a> and share your thoughts here.</p>
