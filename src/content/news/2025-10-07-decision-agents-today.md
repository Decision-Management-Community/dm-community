A brief summary:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Do not use LLMs to make decisions or act as autonomous decision agents. </li>



<li>Instead, leverage LLMs to ingest decision agents and provide clear, contextualized explanations of made decisions to end users. <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRkJTXDromw" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></li>
