<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dr. Harold Boley <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="http://wiki.ruleml.org/index.php/Obituary_for_Harold_Boley" target="_blank">passed away</a> in December 2020. Being a well-known scientist in the semantic web movement and the Chair of <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://wiki.ruleml.org/index.php/Organizational_Structure" target="_blank">RuleML</a> from 2001 to 2020, Harold devoted a lot of efforts to integrate the academic research in the area of rules-based knowledge representation with the commonly used rule engines. In particular, he initiated and actively pushed the integration of <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://declarativeai2021.net/conference/" target="_blank">RuleML</a> and <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/" target="_blank">DecisionCAMP</a> and coined the name &#8220;Declarative AI&#8221; for a summit that annually brings together researchers and practitioners. This year the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://declarativeai2021.net/" target="_blank">Declarative AI</a> will be held in September for the first time without Harold but the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://declarativeai2021.net/rule-challenge/" target="_blank">Rule Challenge</a> will carry his name. <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="http://wiki.ruleml.org/index.php/Obituary_for_Harold_Boley" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
