Jacob Feldman wrote a remembrance of Dr. Harold Boley, who passed away in December 2020. Boley was a
well-known semantic-web researcher and chaired RuleML from 2001 to 2020, spending much of his career
bridging academic rules research with practical, deployable systems — work that shaped a good part of
the standards this community relies on.
