<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You need to create a decision service that decides whether to buy certain stocks or not. Here are the guiding rules:<br>Rule 1: Stock in debt is considered risky.<br>Rule 2: Stocks in fusion with other stocks may be risky.<br>Rule 3: Stock in fusion with a strong stock is not risky.<br>Rule 4: Do not buy risky stocks unless they have a good price.<br>Keep in mind that more rules that conflict with some of these rules can be added later. <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge-may-2025/">Link</a></p>
