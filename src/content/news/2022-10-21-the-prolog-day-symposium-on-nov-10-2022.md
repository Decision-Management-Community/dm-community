<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This Prolog-Day Symposium will present the highlights of the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://prologyear.logicprogramming.org/PrologYear.html" target="_blank">Year of Prolog</a>, celebrating the <strong>50th anniversary of the birth of Prolog</strong>. The Symposium will include the award of the inaugural edition of the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://prologyear.logicprogramming.org/ColmerauerPrize.html" target="_blank">Alain Colmerauer Prize</a> for <em>recent practical accomplishments that highlight the benefits of Prolog-inspired computing for the future</em>, and presents the Prolog education initiative for introducing young people to Prolog. The Symposium will visit the past, present, and future of Prolog and explore its advantages as a language for explainable AI and for computing more generally. Learn more and register <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://prologyear.logicprogramming.org/PrologDay.html" target="_blank">here</a></p>
