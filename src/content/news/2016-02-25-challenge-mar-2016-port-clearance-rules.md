<p>The <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-march-2016/">March-2016 Challenge</a> deals with a decision model that is capable to decide if a ship can enter a Dutch port on a certain date. The rules for this challenge are inspired by the international Ship and Port Facility Security Code.</p>
