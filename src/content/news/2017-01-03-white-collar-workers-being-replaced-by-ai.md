The AI will scan hospital records and other documents to determine insurance payouts, according to <a href="https://translate.googleusercontent.com/translate_c?depth=1&amp;hl=en&amp;prev=search&amp;rurl=translate.google.com&amp;sl=ja&amp;sp=nmt4&amp;u=http://www.fukoku-life.co.jp/about/news/download/20161226.pdf&amp;usg=ALkJrhj96ahbMtMyH8mAIDn8awNFLBt0sQ">a company press release</a>, factoring injuries, patient medical histories, and procedures administered. Automation of these research and data gathering tasks will help the remaining human workers process the final payout faster, the release says.
