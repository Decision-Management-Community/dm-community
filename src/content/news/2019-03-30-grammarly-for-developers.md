A Zurich-based company <a href="https://www.deepcode.ai" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DeepCode</a> claims that they developed a “Grammarly for developers”, a tool that looks a lot like familiar code vetting apps in use today, and is intended to mesh easily into developer’s “normal, everyday workflow”. Their website shows <a href="https://www.deepcode.ai/app/gh/eclipse/che/5be0e29f11fdef73ed4a3da5fe61e3cc0eb3e875/_/%2F/code/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">examples of Java code</a> with automatically generated suggestions for its improvement. An internal analysis shows that DeepCode catches four times more errors than any other tool they tested, without a notably high false-positive rate. <a href="https://sifted.eu/articles/ai-is-coming-for-your-coding-job/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Link</a></p>
