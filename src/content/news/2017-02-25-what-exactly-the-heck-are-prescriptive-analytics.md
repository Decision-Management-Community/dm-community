Many wrongly and incompletely define prescriptive analytics as the what comes after predictive analytics. Our research indicates that prescriptive analytics is not a specific type of analytics, but rather an umbrella term for many types of analytics that can improve decisions. Think of the term “prescriptive” as the goal of all these analytics — to make more effective decisions — rather than a specific analytical technique.</em>&#8221; You may want to get the full report &#8220;<a href="https://www.forrester.com/report/Prescriptive+Analytics+The+Black+Belt+Of+Digital+Decisions/-/E-RES122982" target="_blank">Prescriptive Analytics: The Black Belt Of Digital Decisions</a>&#8220;</p>
