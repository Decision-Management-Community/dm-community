<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The AAAI-23 Constraint Programming and Machine Learning Bridge is part of the AAAI-23 <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://aaai.org/Conferences/AAAI-23/bridge-program-call/" target="_blank">Bridge Program</a>. Bringing together CP (Constraint Programming) and ML (Machine Learning) is an important aspect of the larger goal of integrating Reasoning and Learning. Participants are not expected to have prior experience in both fields, but to have familiarity with each at least at the level of an introductory AI course. The Bridge is designed to provide attendees with a better, broader sense of where we are and where we should be going, and an opportunity to brainstorm, discuss, debate and find collaborators. <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://groups.google.com/g/constraints/c/AVTfdx75nq0?pli=1" target="_blank">Link</a> <a href="https://aaai.org/Conferences/AAAI-23/bridge-program-call/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">AAAI-23</a><br></p>
