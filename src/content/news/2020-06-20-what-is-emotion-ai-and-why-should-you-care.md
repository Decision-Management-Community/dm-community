<p>&#8220;<em>One of the examples involved using drivers’ heartbeats to guide machine learning. (Heart rate is one of the ways we express and experience emotions. Others include at the biological level: changes in body temperature, pupil and blood vessel dilation/constriction and changes in blood flow, breathing, and brain waves, increased/decreased production of saliva, hormones and digestive enzymes, and so on.) Mr. McDuff’s team used drivers’ emotional responses as expressed through their heartbeat in training a neural network model to drive a car. The result? The model was able to drive a vehicle longer than the state-of-the-art model without emotions.</em>&#8220;</p>
