Bit Bros LLC has published a new **Decision Factory** framework for designing recurring operational decision systems. The architecture organizes the decision flow into five stages: **Sense → Predict → Price → Decide → Commit**.

The first four stages are designed to run unattended. A person enters at **Commit** to approve or override the recommendation, while outcomes and overrides feed back into the system as a learning signal. The framework puts the economic objective, uncertainty, automated decision logic, and human judgment into one closed decision loop rather than treating them as separate analytics activities.

Explore the framework at [TheDecisionFactory.com](https://thedecisionfactory.com/), including the detailed [Decision Flow](https://thedecisionfactory.com/decision-architecture/).
