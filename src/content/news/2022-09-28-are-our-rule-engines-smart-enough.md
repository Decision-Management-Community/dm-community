<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On Sep 27, 2022 we had several discussions related to this question during the ongoing <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2022.wordpress.com/" target="_blank">DecisionCAMP</a>. Are we happy with where we are or should we raise the bar trying to come closer to our long-standing &#8220;big&#8221; objective when a subject matter expert defines a business problem and our smart engine comes up with a decision? In my <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O0v_pd3kduw" target="_blank">presentation</a> I presented a good enough solution of a real-world problem which still forced a business user to define nested loops to specify how to find a decision. I asked if it was really necessary? Then we discussed a more general question during our expert panel: watch this <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3neMcVAIBQk" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">discussion</a> and provide your comments.</p>
