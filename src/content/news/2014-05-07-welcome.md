<p style="text-align:right;"><em>For practitioners by practitioners!</em></p>
<p><em>Decision Management (DM)</em> quickly becomes the critical methodological and technological approach to support decision making processes in different industries. Today thousands of business analysts and developers, technology vendors and consultants, decision makers at all enterprise levels become DM practitioners who use Decision Management technologies such as <em>Business Rules</em>, <em>Predictive Analytics</em>, <em>Business Intelligence</em> and <em>Decision Optimization</em>.<!--more--></p>
<p>This website is intended to provide and keep up-to-date various information sources for the Decision Management Community. It is the place where DM practitioners may share practical knowledge about:</p>
<ul>
<li><a title="Products" href="http://dmcommunity.org/tools/">Decision Management Tools</a> classified into Live Catalogs that support feature-by-feature comparisons (using product profiles maintained by DM vendors)</li>
<li><a title="Decision Models" href="http://dmcommunity.org/decision-models/">Decision Models</a> for different lines of business that can be analyzed, executed and/or used as prototypes for custom decision models (provided by DM practitioners)</li>
<li><a title="Standards" href="http://dmcommunity.org/standards/">DM Standards</a> with examples and recommendations for their real-world application</li>
<li>Upcoming <a title="Events" href="http://dmcommunity.org/events/">Decision Management Events</a></li>
<li>Various <a title="Resources" href="http://dmcommunity.org/resources/">Decision Management Resources</a> including the most popular Discussion Groups and Web Links.</li>
</ul>
<p>You are very welcome to add your own decision models, tools, events, resources. We encourage all practitioners from vendors to end-users to post news, web links, comments or articles to this Community site. <span style="color:#333333;">Please send your suggestions to </span><a href="mailto:DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com" target="_blank">DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com</a>.</p>
<p>Visit, comment, participate!</p>
