Solvometry is developing an early-alpha technology at the intersection of mathematical optimization, AI, and machine learning. The team is looking for organizations that use optimization to make operational decisions and want to explore whether new approaches can help find high-quality solutions sooner and use compute more effectively.

**Design partners receive free early access** in exchange for collaborating on evaluations and providing feedback. No performance improvements are claimed in advance; the goal is to test them rigorously on representative workloads.

If your organization is interested in participating, visit [solvometry.com](https://solvometry.com) or email [adam@bitbrosdata.com](mailto:adam@bitbrosdata.com).
