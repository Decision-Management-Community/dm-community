<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A group of researchers (many of whom are associated with <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://declarativeai2021.net/" target="_blank">Declarative AI</a> and indirectly with <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://dmcommunity.org/decisioncamp/" target="_blank">DecisionCAMP</a>) just published the manifesto &#8220;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.12855v1.pdf" target="_blank"><strong>Augmented Business Process Management Systems</strong></a>&#8220;: &#8220;<em>While traditional BPMSs encode pre-defined flows and rules, an ABPMS augmented by AI is <strong>able to reason</strong> about the current state of the process (or across several processes) to determine a course of action that improves the performance of the process</em>.&#8221; This manifesto outlines the lifecycle of processes within an ABPMS, discusses core characteristics of an ABPMS, and derives a set of challenges to realize systems with these characteristics. <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.12855v1.pdf" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
