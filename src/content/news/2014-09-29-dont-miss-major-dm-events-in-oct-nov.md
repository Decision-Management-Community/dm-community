<p>Two major Decision Management events will take place in October/November 2014 in the US:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="http://www.decision-camp.com/" target="_blank">Decision CAMP 2014, October 13-15</a> </strong> San Jose, CA &#8211; many presentations open <strong>technical</strong> implementation details (registration is free)</li>
<li><strong><a href="http://www.businessrulesforum.com/" target="_blank">BBC 2014, November 2-6</a></strong> Fort Lauterdale, FL &#8211; majority of the presentations oriented to <strong>business</strong> analysts</li>
</ul>
