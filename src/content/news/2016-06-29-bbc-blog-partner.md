The community's site became an official blog partner of Building Business Capability (BBC), the annual
conference covering business analysis, architecture, process, rules, and decision management. It was a
recognition of the community's role in the wider business-analysis and decision-management ecosystem,
beyond just the DMN/rules-engine crowd.
