<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This month we asked our readers to use their favorite BR/DM tools or just common sense to solve a simple puzzle &#8220;<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-june-2021/">Which box contains the gold?</a>&#8221; The very first quick solution by Jan Vanthienen uses decision tables to classify different possibilities. It&#8217;s interesting that <em>these decision tables don&#8217;t need to be executable</em> as their design already resolves the problem.  Of course, as Jan wrote, &#8220;a general formulation of the problem will be better&#8221; and probably a larger problem with more objects and known relationships between them (e.g. notorious <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zebra_Puzzle" target="_blank">Zebra Puzzle</a>) would be more difficult to solve without a tool. Let&#8217;s wait for more solutions. <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-june-2021/">Link</a></p>
