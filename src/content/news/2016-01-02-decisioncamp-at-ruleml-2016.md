<p>This year for the first time <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/ruleml2016/decisioncamp" target="_blank">DecisionCAMP</a> will be hosted by the International Web Rule Symposium (<a href="http://2016.ruleml.org/" target="_blank">RuleML</a>-2016), a leading international conference well-known for building bridges between academia and industry in the field of rules and its applications. RuleML-2016 will be help on July 6-9, 2016 at <a href="http://www.cs.stonybrook.edu/" target="_blank">Stony Brook University</a>, New York, USA – see <a href="http://2016.ruleml.org/venue-and-accommodation" target="_blank">Venue &amp; Accommodation</a>.</p>
<p>The event “<strong><a href="http://2016.ruleml.org/decisioncamp" target="_blank">DecisionCAMP at RuleML</a></strong>” is sponsored by <a href="http://dmcommunity.org/" target="_blank">DMCommunity.org</a>. It will be held on <strong>July 7, 2016</strong> <!--more-->with presentations from leading decision management authorities, vendors, and practitioners. We expect that this event will summarize the current state of the DM practice, the use of the DMN standard, real-world business cases and their solutions provided using various DM tools.</p>
<p>Become a presenter and/or simply join DM practitioners like you – the Call for Presentations can be found <a href="http://2016.ruleml.org/decisioncamp" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>
