<p>Dan Selman and Andy Ritchie from IBM just published an interesting paper &#8220;<a href="https://developer.ibm.com/odm/docs/odm-capabilities/odm-advanced-decision-server-insights/event-driven-architecture-and-decision-management/" target="_blank">Event Driven Architecture and Decision Management</a>&#8220;. It is for enterprise architects considering putting in place an event driven architecture that will incorporate aspects of event processing, analytics, situation detection and decision-making. <!--more-->It is interesting that their loop</p>
<h3>Receive Events → Detect Situation → Decide Response → Take Action</h3>
<p>combines &#8220;Detect &amp; Decide&#8221; in one component by integrating CEP and BRMS components:</p>
