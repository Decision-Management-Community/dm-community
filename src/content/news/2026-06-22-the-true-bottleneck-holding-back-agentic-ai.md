<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Prof. Marlon Dumas posted: &#8220;<em>Lately, we’ve seen a shift from AI that just thinks to AI that acts. But as autonomous agents start making decisions, buying services, and moving across internal systems, we face a critical legal and security hurdle: <strong>If an AI agent acts under my personal or corporate identity, I&#8217;m fully liable for everything it does!</strong></em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>Would we hand a blank, signed check and your power of attorney to a software program? Of course not! Yet, that’s exactly the risk profile we create when agents inherit the &#8220;unlimited liability&#8221; of their owner. If a company faces existential risk every time an agent executes a workflow, the natural response will be to heavily restrict what agents are allowed to do</em>.&#8221; <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/search-identity-true-bottleneck-holding-back-agentic-ai-marlon-dumas-oy50e/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
