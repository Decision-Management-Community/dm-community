<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It is interesting to look at the latest Decision Intelligence trends from the <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jacobfeldmanopenrules_dont-program-a-system-educate-it-activity-7290891959731183616-VVDy?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=member_desktop" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">2015 perspective</a>: “<em><strong>You don’t program a system, you educate it</strong>. Rather than coding into the system, you merely provide a large set of training examples.<br>Business people will continue to enhance and manage their decision model by doing the following:<br>&#8211; Adding more business concepts and decision variables<br>&#8211; Covering more complex relationships between decision variables inside business rules<br>&#8211; Defining and executing more complex test cases.</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>This way decision management becomes continuing education of already working decisioning system!</em>&#8220;</p>
