<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The year 2026 is swiftly approaching. We&#8217;ve just published a new website for <a href="https://decisioncamp2026.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">DecisionCAMP-2026</a>. This is a major annual event devoted to Decision Intelligence Technologies. It is scheduled to take place <strong>online </strong>from <strong>August 26 to 28, 2026</strong>. It is organized by the <a href="http://dmcommunity.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Decision Management Community</strong></a> and will take place concurrently with the <a href="https://2026.declarativeai.net/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Declarative AI 2026</strong></a> conference. The <a href="https://decisioncamp2026.wordpress.com/free-registration/">Registration </a>is FREE. <a href="https://decisioncamp2026.wordpress.com/call-for-presentations/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Call for Presentations</a> is open &#8211; you may submit your abstract via <a href="https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=decisioncamp2026" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">EasyChair</a>. <a href="mailto:decisionmanagementcommunity@gmail.com">Contact us</a> if you plan to present and have any questions.</p>
