<p>This <a href="http://www.fico.com/en/blogs/analytics-optimization/unlocking-the-science-of-sequential-decisions/?utm_source=feedburner&amp;utm_medium=feed&amp;utm_campaign=Feed%3A+EnterpriseDecisionManagementWeblog+%28Decision+Management+Blog%29" target="_blank">FICO&#8217;s post</a> describes how a combination of Analytics and Optimization helps to predict and compare likely future outcomes of alternative actions and find the best decisions given business objectives. For example, a marketer may decide today to spend $70 in acquisition costs to turn a promising prospect into a customer. If she becomes a customer, then, depending on her first 3 months of shopping behavior, another decision will be taken whether to spend $30 in incentives to entice her into a loyalty program. Sequential decision analytics can trigger individual dialogues with such customers</p>
