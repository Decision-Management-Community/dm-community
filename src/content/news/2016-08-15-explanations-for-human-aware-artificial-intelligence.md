<p><a href="https://freuder.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Eugene Freuder</a> will present position papers &#8220;<a href="http://cp2016.a4cp.org/program/workshops/ws-cpai16-papers/CPAIW_2016_paper_8.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Explanations for Human-Aware Constraint Programming (CP)</a>&#8221; and &#8220;<a href="http://cp2016.a4cp.org/program/workshops/ws-cpai16-papers/CPAIW_2016_paper_9.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CP and AI</a>&#8221; at <a href="http://cp2016.a4cp.org/program/workshops/ws-cpai16.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CP 2016</a> on Sep 5-9 at Toulouse, France. &#8220;<em>A key aspect of human-awareness is the ability of our machines to explain their behavior and their decisions. Accordingly we focus here on explanation in CP for users. The most attention has been paid to programs providing explanations for themselves, to enable them to function more efficiently. There has been work on providing explanations to programmers to facilitate development or debugging. There has even been some work on users providing explanations to programs, to facilitate problem acquisition.</em> &#8220;</p>
