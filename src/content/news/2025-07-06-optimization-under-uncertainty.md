<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Optimization under uncertainty needs to be embedded within your business as a living system. Its success is measured not by solver convergence or benchmark accuracy, but by decisions that consistently align operational realities with financial objectives under real-world volatility.</p>



<!--more-->



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" id="ember1295">Adam&#8217;s recommended infrastructure includes:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Data ingestion → signal extraction → belief updates → policy execution, creating a continuous flow from raw data to action.</li>



<li>Feedback loops to measure decision outcomes and improve policies systematically over time.</li>



<li>Ownership: ensuring teams are accountable for system performance in production, not just offline model metrics or slide-deck KPIs.</li>
</ul>
