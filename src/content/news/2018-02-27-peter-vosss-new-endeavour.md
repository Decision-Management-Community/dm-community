<p>A well-known AI expert <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/vosspeter/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Peter Voss</a> just started a new <a href="http://aigo.ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">business</a> with this vision:<br />
&#8220;<em>Imagine a super-smart Alexa/ Siri/ Google Assistant-like app or device that actually remembers what you told it previously; can hold lengthy conversations; can interact with other assistants, devices, and apps; and that can learn new facts, preferences and skills via English conversation.<br /> </em><!--more--><em>Now imagine that this assistant isn’t just available in one domain, but can assist you using complex software, allow you to control and monitor your home, car, and other devices, in addition to handling a wide range of personal and business requests.</em><br />
<em> Finally, imagine that this personal AI not only gets smarter as it learns from you, but also as it interacts with other people (and AIs) in your network. Furthermore, it constantly acquires new knowledge, skills, and capabilities via ongoing development</em>.&#8221;<!--more--><!--more--><!--more--></p>
