<p>A number of vendors supporting the DMN standard keeps growing. Today Sapiens announced that its product &#8220;Sapiens DECISION&#8221; now supports the DMN &#8211; read more <a href="http://www.sapiens.com/news/sapiens-decision-announces-support-for-decision-model-and-notation-dmn/" target="_blank">here</a>. Sapiens, as well as other vendors not yet included into the <a href="http://openjvm.jvmhost.net/DMNtools/" target="_blank">DMN Tools Catalog</a> may add their product profile by filling up this <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/tools/adding-your-product-to-catalogs/">request</a>.  Remember that the Catalog allows readers to compare different products feature-by-feature.</p>
