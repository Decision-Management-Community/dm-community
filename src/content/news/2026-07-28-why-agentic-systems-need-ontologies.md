Prof. Frank Coyle makes the case that AI agents produce better, more explainable decisions when they're
grounded in an explicit ontology rather than left to infer structure from prompts alone — an argument
that lines up closely with this community's long-standing preference for explicit, auditable decision
models over black-box behavior.
