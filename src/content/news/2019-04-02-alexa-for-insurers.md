It also offers actionable advice on common home and auto queries. Insurance carrier <a href="https://www.aviva.co.uk/alexa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Aviva</a> uses Alexa to answer questions about insurance and regards Alexa as the future of their customer interactions. <a href="https://www.safeco.com/customer-resources/mobile-voice-apps/alexa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Safeco</a> introduced an insurance advisor skill for Alexa, allowing customers to simply ask Alexa around 100 common customer questions about insurance policies. Safeco is also looking to offer customized insurance products directly via Alexa.
