<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Stefaan Lambrecht, our presenter at the upcoming <a href="https://decisioncamp2026.wordpress.com/program/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DecisionCAMP</a>, just wrote: &#8220;<em>For years, insurers have been trapped in a frustrating pattern. Business teams generate innovative ideas, but implementation depends on lengthy development cycles. As a result, many promising concepts are postponed, reduced in scope, or abandoned before they are ever tested with real data.</em>&#8221; Then he describes the creation of an AI Agent when an insurer wanted to introduce a new coverage option. </p>



<!--more-->



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">&#8220;<em>Within a few minutes, an AI Agent was configured and instructed —in plain language— to act as an experienced travel claims specialist. We then created a simple business flow that asked the AI to analyse the policy conditions, the customer contract and the claim statement, and to determine all information needed for a claims assessment. No coding was involved.</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>Was the policy active? Was the customer covered? Did the incident occur during the insured period? Was the object eligible for the new Platinum treatment? Were all special conditions satisfied?</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The AI extracted the information and explained its reasoning. The final acceptance and compensation decision was then applied automatically according to the insurer’s business rules</em>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The entire solution was deployed and executed in about <strong>twenty minutes</strong>.</em>&#8221; <a href="https://stefaanlambrecht.substack.com/p/insurance-innovation-at-the-speed">Link</a></p>
