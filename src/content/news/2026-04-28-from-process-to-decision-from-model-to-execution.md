<p class="has-small-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Most BPM initiatives fail.<br>Not because of tooling.<br>Not because of lack of effort.</p>



<p class="has-small-font-size wp-block-paragraph">But because they focus on the wrong thing. They optimise flows…<br>While the real problem sits elsewhere:<br>👉 Decisions — invisible, unmanaged, and owned by no one.</p>
