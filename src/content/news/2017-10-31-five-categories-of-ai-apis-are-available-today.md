This Forrester&#8217;s <a href="https://go.forrester.com/blogs/ai-apis-dead-simple-ai/?utm_source=Twitter&amp;utm_campaign=research_social&amp;utm_content=blog_carlsson" target="_blank" rel="noopener">article</a> lists 5 categories of AI APIs available today:<!--more--></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Vision</strong>: making applications <span style="text-decoration:underline;"><em>see</em></span> – convert speech to text and text to speech, identify the speaker, and analyze speech for insights</li>
<li><strong>Speech</strong>: making applications <span style="text-decoration:underline;"><em>listen </em></span>and <span style="text-decoration:underline;"><em>speak</em></span></li>
<li><strong>Language</strong>: making applications <span style="text-decoration:underline;"><em>understand </em></span>– recognize entities, extract intent, topics, and sentiment, translate text</li>
<li><strong>Conversation</strong>: making applications <span style="text-decoration:underline;"><em>interact </em></span>– determine intent, follow up with questions, and then take appropriate actions</li>
<li><strong>Knowledge</strong>: making applications <span style="text-decoration:underline;"><em>encyclopedic </em></span>– find relevant information either through greater search functions, by accessing enhanced data, or <span style="text-decoration:underline;"><em>by returning decisions based on submitted and/or external data</em></span></li>
</ul>
