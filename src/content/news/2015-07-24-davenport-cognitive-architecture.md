<p>This month Tom Davenport posted a new article at LinkedIn &#8220;<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/building-your-cognitive-technology-tom-davenport?trk=eml-b2_content_ecosystem_digest-recommended_articles-237-null&amp;midToken=AQFHYWW_SEhf5w&amp;fromEmail=fromEmail&amp;ut=0Fyp3iDcfjECQ1" target="_blank">Building Your Cognitive Technology</a>&#8221; exploring cognitive technologies that &#8220;automate aspects of decision-making processes&#8221;. He shares key attributes of the emerging cognitive architectures:<!--more--></p>
<ul>
<li>Able to handle a variety of data types</li>
<li>Capable of learning</li>
<li>Humans and cognitive technologies working together</li>
<li>Ease of updating and modification</li>
<li>Strong reporting capabilities</li>
<li>General IT hygiene</li>
</ul>
