Analytics researcher Tom Davenport published a LinkedIn piece, "Building Your Cognitive Technology,"
examining the emerging class of tools that automate parts of the decision-making process. He laid out a
short set of defining attributes for these "cognitive architectures" — an early, practitioner-focused
attempt to describe what would later be called decision intelligence and AI-assisted decisioning.
