<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Andreessen">Marc Andreessen</a> wrote: &#8220;<strong>What AI <em>is</em></strong>: The application of mathematics and software code to teach computers how to understand, synthesize, and generate knowledge in ways similar to how people do it. AI is a computer program like any other – it runs, takes input, processes, and generates output. AI’s output is useful across a wide range of fields, ranging from coding to medicine to law to the creative arts. It is owned by people and controlled by people, like any other technology.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>What AI <em>isn’t</em></strong>: Killer software and robots that will spring to life and decide to murder the human race or otherwise ruin everything, like you see in <a href="https://movieweb.com/best-killer-robot-horror-movies-ranked/">the movies</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>What AI&nbsp;<em>could be</em></strong>: A way to make everything we care about better.&#8221; <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://pmarca.substack.com/p/why-ai-will-save-the-world" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
