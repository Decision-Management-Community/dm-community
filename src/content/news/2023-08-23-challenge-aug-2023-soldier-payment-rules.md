<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We decided to bring back <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-aug-2023-2/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Challenge “Soldier Payment Rules”</a> which in 2017 received only two solutions. This is a good chance to demonstrate the expressive power of your DMN or other decision management tools. The proper decision model is supposed to assemble a payment timeline for a soldier that shows his/her hourly pay rate in any given time period. The payment rates depend on soldier’s rank, profession, service type, unit, and participation in combat. Submit your solutions to <a href="mailto:DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com">DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com</a>. <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-aug-2023/">Link</a></p>
