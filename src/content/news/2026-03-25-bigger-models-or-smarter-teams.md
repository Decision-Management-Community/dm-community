<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>Key Shifts to Consider:<br><br>• Logic over Intuition: While models excel at rapid pattern recognition (System 1), they struggle with slow, deliberate reasoning (System 2). Teamwork can effectively bridge this gap.<br><br>• The Power of Debate: In experiments involving the Collatz conjecture, no individual model succeeded. Success was achieved only when models cross-checked each other, with one proposing and another verifying.<br><br>• The &#8220;Yes-Man&#8221; Problem: While hallucinations in AI are concerning, sycophancy (where AI merely agrees) poses a greater risk. We need an AI capable of saying &#8220;No!!!&#8221;<br><br>• Intelligence is Social: As Chang articulates, &#8220;Intelligence is not a property of individual agents but of regulated interaction.&#8221;</em></p>
