The first federal court ruling on whether training generative AI on copyrighted material is fair use
landed as a mixed outcome for both tech companies and content creators, according to the attorneys
tracking it. It's the kind of legal-landscape shift that will eventually shape what any vendor building
LLM-based decisioning tools can and can't train on.
