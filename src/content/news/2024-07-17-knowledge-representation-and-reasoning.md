<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dan Selman wrote: &#8220;The key to Knowledge Representation and Reasoning is building your ontology. It doesn&#8217;t matter if you are reasoning using a Knowledge Graph, a rules engine or a relational database — you need to do the work of understanding the concepts in your domain, and their properties and relationships.<br><br>That is fundamentally different to &#8220;find me chunks of text similar to this chunk of text&#8221;.<br><br>I think folks are getting confused because an LLM can be a useful aid in understand the concepts in a domain, extracting instances of known domain concepts from text, and searching for text based on vector embedding, and they are a Swiss Army knife for data format transformation and text generation.<br><br>Let&#8217;s use the right tool for the right problem. LLMs are great at many things, but reasoning is not one of them.&#8221; <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dselman_knowledgegraphs-llms-graphrag-activity-7219327831833542657-O1M-?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=member_desktop" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></p>
