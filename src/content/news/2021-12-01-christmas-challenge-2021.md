<p class="wp-block-paragraph">2022 is around the corner, and Santa again creates a plan for his elves to determine how reindeer will pull his sleigh. Our <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge-dec-2021/">Challenge Dec-2021</a> is based on Santa’s plan described by&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.puzzazz.com/shenk" target="_blank">Mike Shenk</a>:</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><span style="color:red;">Twas the night before Christmas, and at the North Pole<br>The last-minute planning was taking its toll.<br>As Santa was hastily making a scheme<br>For the placement of deer in his sleigh-pulling team..</span></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We expect our readers will present Santa’s plan as a nice-looking decision model that determines the order of the reindeer and send their solution using any tool (or no tools) to&nbsp;<a href="mailto:DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com">DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com</a>. <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge-dec-2021/">Link</a></p>
