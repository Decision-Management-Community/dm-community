<p class="wp-block-paragraph">WIRED just published <a href="https://www.wired.com/story/meet-bard-googles-answer-to-chatgpt/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">comments</a> on today&#8217;s announcement that Google will roll out a chatbot named Bard “in the coming weeks.” The launch appears to be a response to ChatGPT, the sensationally popular&nbsp;artificial intelligence&nbsp;chatbot developed by startup OpenAI with funding from Microsoft. Sundar Pichai, Google’s CEO,&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://blog.google/technology/ai/bard-google-ai-search-updates/" target="_blank">wrote in a blog post</a>&nbsp;that Bard is already available to “trusted testers” and designed to put the “breadth of the world’s knowledge” behind a conversational interface.</p>
