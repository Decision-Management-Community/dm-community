<p>What constitutes a good decision table? There are many recommendations provided by well-known experts such as  Prof. Jan Vanthienen.  But it&#8217;s always a good idea to see at least one specific example and to analyze its different representations. So, we published a new <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-jan-2016/">Challenge January-2016</a> that specifies a notorious decision model &#8220;Calculate Vacation Days&#8221;.  We expect that DM vendors and/or consultants will provide their best representations of the proper decision table. Then we will ask Prof. Vanthienen to provide his comparative analysis of different submitted decision tables.</p>
