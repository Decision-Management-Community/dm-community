<p>Besides the classic business rules projects, ACTICO Modeler now also provides DMN projects. Users continue to benefit from the well-known, intuitive modeling approach. Some exemplary features are:</p>
<ul>
<li>Building Decision Requirements Diagrams (DRDs) using drag &amp; drop</li>
<li>Support for multiple DRDs in one decision model</li>
<li>Support for all boxed expressions and full FEEL</li>
<li>Validation of DMN models and listing of detected problems in a problem view</li>
<li>Transparent definition of decision services</li>
</ul>
<p>Further information can be found at the ACTICO <a href="https://www.actico.com/en/products/decision-modeling" target="_blank" rel="noopener">website</a></p>
