<p class="wp-block-paragraph">These days everybody talks about AI. If you are a person who &#8220;walks the talk&#8221; and want to share your AI experience with practitioners like you, <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/" target="_blank">DecisionCAMP</a> is the right place to do it. DecisionCAMP is an annual event devoted to&nbsp;<strong>Decision Intelligence</strong>&nbsp;technologies that bring AI into the hands of business users. DecisionCAMP presenters explain how to build&nbsp;Intelligent Decision Services&nbsp;and integrate&nbsp;them into modern enterprise architectures. This year DecisionCAMP will be held online on Sep 18-20. To become a presenter, submit an abstract of your presentation by <strong>June 1</strong> &#8211; see <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/call-for-presentations/" target="_blank">Call for Presentations</a>.</p>
