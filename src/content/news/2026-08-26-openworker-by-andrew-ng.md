Andrew Ng announced OpenWorker, an open source agent that doesn't just chat but completes tasks on your laptop using the tools you use every day — your files, repos, Slack, calendar, and more. It carries tasks through from start to finish: your code reviewed for vulnerabilities with fixes ready to go, your cloud configuration checked, a polished document delivered. It checks in before important actions, so you stay in charge.
