<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An interesting event &#8220;<strong><a href="https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-25/bridge-ai-orms/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bridge: AI+ORMS</a></strong>&#8221; will occur on <strong>February 25-26, 2025</strong> as a part of the 39th Annual AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Philadelphia USA. &#8220;<em>The goal of this bridge program is to unite AI and OR/MS practitioners and researchers to improve trustworthy decision-making in key socio-technical areas such as supply chains, healthcare, crisis management, homeland security, robotics, wildlife conservation, medicine, transportation, and finance. It aims to equip them with better tools by familiarizing them with each other’s techniques and domains and bring the disciplines together to advance the research and application at the intersection of AI and OR/MS so as to improve decision-making</em>.&#8221; <a href="https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-25/bridge-ai-orms/">Link</a></p>
