<p>One of the main advantages of decision modeling standardization is a potential interchange of decision models between different DMN-compliant tools. DMN 1.1 includes a new interchange format – you may find its description in the Bruce Silver’s book “DMN Method and Style”. However, we still have not seen any example of a decision model presented in DMN XML and executed by different tools. This new DMCommunity.org <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-april-2016/">challenge &#8220;DMN Interchange&#8221;</a> is intended to at least partially close this gap.</p>
