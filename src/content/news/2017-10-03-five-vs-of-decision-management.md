<p>A new article by <a href="http://martijnzoet.com/2017/10/01/decision-management-the-choice-between-a-human-or-a-machine/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Koen Smit</a> discusses 5 characteristics of operational decision management:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Volume</strong> corresponds to the number of times a decision is made within a certain time period</li>
<li><strong>Velocity</strong> represents how fast a decision must be taken</li>
<li><strong>Variety</strong> corresponds to the variety in execution paths of a decision</li>
<li><strong>Veracity</strong> denotes the accuracy with which a decision should be taken</li>
<li><strong>Value</strong> indicates the value that the decision represents for the organization.</li>
</ol>
