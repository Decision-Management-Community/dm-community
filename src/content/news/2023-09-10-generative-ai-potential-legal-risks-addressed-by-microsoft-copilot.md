<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Customers of generative AI tools are concerned about the <a href="https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/legal-issues-presented-generative-ai">risk of IP infringement claims</a> if they use the produced output. This is understandable, given recent public inquiries by authors and artists regarding how their own work is being used in conjunction with AI models and services.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">&#8220;To address this customer concern, Microsoft is announcing our new Copilot Copyright Commitment. As customers ask whether they can use Microsoft’s Copilot services and the output they generate without worrying about copyright claims, we are providing a straightforward answer: yes, you can, and if you are challenged on copyright grounds, we will assume responsibility for the potential legal risks involved.&#8221; <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2023/09/07/copilot-copyright-commitment-ai-legal-concerns/" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
