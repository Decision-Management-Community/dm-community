A look back at Charles Forgy's 1982 paper "Rete: A Fast Algorithm for the Many Pattern/Many Object
Pattern Match Problem," marking 40 years since its publication. The Rete algorithm remains the matching
engine underneath many production rules engines in use today, making this one of the more direct
technical-lineage pieces in the archive.
