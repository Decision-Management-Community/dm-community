Having said that, the breadth and depth of decision intelligence technologies are so broad that specialized <strong>AI Decisioning Agents</strong> can be much more sophisticated and subject to more direct human governance. Why? 1) Take a decisioning technique like mixed integer programming. That is super valuable. Sure an Agent can call a &#8220;tool&#8221; to do that but it also requires deep integration and configuration. 2) AI Decisioning platforms also enable human decision-logic also known as rules. They provide pretty nice tools that business experts can use to combine these rules. And, there is also decision optimization, champion challenger, a/b testing, simulation and more.<br><br>It is true that general AI agents could gain these capabilities, but I think there is a strong case for AI Decision Agents that in turn would be used as a &#8220;tool&#8221; by other AI Agents because of the human tooling to configure, change, and monitor those decisions.</em>&#8221; <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7295123392708378626?commentUrn=urn%3Ali%3Acomment%3A%28activity%3A7295123392708378626%2C7295652595061112832%29&amp;replyUrn=urn%3Ali%3Acomment%3A%28activity%3A7295123392708378626%2C7295806054037086209%29&amp;dashCommentUrn=urn%3Ali%3Afsd_comment%3A%287295652595061112832%2Curn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A7295123392708378626%29&amp;dashReplyUrn=urn%3Ali%3Afsd_comment%3A%287295806054037086209%2Curn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A7295123392708378626%29" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></p>
