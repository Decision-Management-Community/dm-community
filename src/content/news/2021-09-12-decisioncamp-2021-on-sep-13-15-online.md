<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/" target="_blank">13th DecisionCAMP</a> will be held next week on Sep 13-15 with the major scheme &#8220;INTELLIGENT DECISION SERVICES&#8221;. The <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/registration/" target="_blank">registration</a> is free and all registered attendees receive the proper Zoom URL or may get it on the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp.slack.com/archives/C02BD1TB94Y" target="_blank">Slack channel</a> where they may ask questions and receive answers during and after the sessions. Each day starts at 9:00 AM EDT (15:00 CEST). There will be 15 <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/program/" target="_blank">presentations</a> each starting on the top of the hour &#8211; see the complete <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/schedule/" target="_blank">Schedule</a>. There will be two <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/keynote/" target="_blank">keynotes</a> shared with the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://declarativeai2021.net/" target="_blank">Declarative AI</a>. To watch keynotes at 12:00 PM EDT you will need to temporarily switch to the URL by clicking on the Keynote&#8217;s &#8220;<span style="text-decoration:underline">watch</span>&#8221; at the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/schedule/" target="_blank">Schedule</a>. At the end of the Sep 13 and 14 we will run two <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/program/#Panels" target="_blank">interactive panels</a> &#8220;Ask a Practitioner&#8221; and &#8220;Ask a Vendor&#8221;.  Don&#8217;t miss the major Decision Management event of the year! <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2021.wordpress.com/registration/" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
