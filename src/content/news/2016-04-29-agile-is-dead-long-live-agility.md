<p>It started with this <a href="https://pragdave.me/blog/2014/03/04/time-to-kill-agile/">2014 post</a> by Dave Thomas, one of the creators of the <a href="http://agilemanifesto.org/" target="_blank">Manifesto for Agile Software Development</a>. Dave proposed to retire the word &#8220;Agile&#8221;. To learn why, you may listen his recent <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqz8ND-N1hc" target="_blank">presentation</a>. Below are a few interesting quotes:<!--more--></p>
<p><em>&#8220;Some people think they are important. Don&#8217;t let them tell you what to do. &#8220;</em></p>
<p><em>&#8220;Agile experts give you rules of how to do stuff. This is wrong because No Rules are Universal. All rules need context.&#8221;</em></p>
<p><em>&#8220;How do you know what to do? The simple answer is: you don&#8217;t!&#8221;</em></p>
