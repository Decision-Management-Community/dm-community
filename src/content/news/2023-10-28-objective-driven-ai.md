<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Objective-Driven AI refers to the idea proposed by Yann LeCun of <strong>creating AI systems that are explicitly designed and constrained to optimize particular objectives</strong>. The key aspects are: Architecture with different modules — perception, world model, action planning, cost functions. Today LeCun tweeted: &#8220;<em>Instead of scaling current systems 100x, which will go nowhere, we need to make these Objective-Driven AI architectures work</em>.&#8221; Watch his MIT talk &#8220;<a href="https://lnkd.in/eY7x_mPr" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Objective-Driven AI: towards AI systems that can learn, remember, plan, reason, have common sense, yet are steerable and safe</a>&#8220;.
