<ul>
<li><em><strong>Decision Requirements Diagrams</strong> structure decision problems, break them into coherent pieces. They show where data is used and what knowledge assets (policies, regulations, best practices) are involved.</em></li>
<li><em><strong>Decision tables</strong> specify the logic for most of the decisions on the diagram using simple constructs</em>.&#8221;</li>
</ul>
