<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Decision logic is not software code. As a result, you can’t be satisfied with testing tools and techniques that software developers use. Testing decision requires a different approach, different techniques, and different tools. Granted, ensuring that your decision service can compile is useful. But, the end goal is really to ensure that your decision logic complies with your business objectives. On July 22 &nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.sparklinglogic.com/team/" target="_blank">Carole-Ann Berlioz</a> from Sparkling Logic will explore what makes decisions different, how to test them, and how to automate regression tests.&nbsp; <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.sparklinglogic.com/webinar/testing-decisions/" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
