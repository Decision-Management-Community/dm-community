<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the real world not all rules can be satisfied and we may consider them as preferences. For example, our June-2023 Challenge “<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-june-2023/">Miss Manners</a>” used well-tuned <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://github.com/DMCommunity/dmcommunity_shared/blob/master/MissMannersData.xls" target="_blank">data</a> with equal numbers of males and females at each party. Of course, <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://github.com/DMCommunity/dmcommunity_shared/blob/master/MissMannersAdvancedData.xls" target="_blank">real data</a> is not like this, but we still want to help Miss Manners to seat all guests while sticking to her rules as much as possible. So, the seating arrangement “<em>boy-girl-boy-girl and each guest has someone on the left or right with a common hobby</em>” becomes <strong>not a Rule but a Preference</strong>. Let&#8217;s see how modern decision intelligence tools can address this advanced version of  the notorious &#8220;Miss Manners&#8221; benchmark.  <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-july-2023/">Link</a> </p>
