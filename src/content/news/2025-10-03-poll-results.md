<p class="wp-block-paragraph">During DecisionCAMP-2025, we conducted the poll &#8220;<strong><a href="https://decisioncamp2025.wordpress.com/poll/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Using LLM-based tools in the Decision Intelligence Context</a></strong>&#8220;. This poll pertained solely to <em>Operational Repetitive Business Decisions</em>. It contained 13 questions about the use of LLMs for the various decision automation tasks. Here are the strongest choices (with more than 45%):</p>



<!--more-->



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Already Use</strong>:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Discovery of business concepts, decision variables, and relationships between them</li>



<li>Generation of decisioning logic in the form of business rules and decision tables oriented to business analysts</li>



<li>Generation of decisioning logic in the form of executable<strong> </strong>code oriented to software developers</li>



<li>Generation of code snippets used for the implementation of small logical parts and their subsequent inclusion in a decision model</li>



<li>Conversion of the decision output, including explanations, into a more friendly business format</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Plan to use</strong>:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Deploying traditional decision services as AI Agents</li>



<li>Using MCP (Model Context Protocol)</li>



<li>Automatic Invocation of AI Agents</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Will not use:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Generation of a ready-to-be-executed decision model capable of producing business decisions &#8220;without a man in the loop&#8221;</li>
</ul>
