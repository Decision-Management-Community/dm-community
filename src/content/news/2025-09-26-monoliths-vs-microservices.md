<p class="wp-block-paragraph">&#8220;For a decade, the dominant Silicon Valley mantra has been:&nbsp;<em>monoliths don’t scale; microservices do.</em>&nbsp;Netflix itself helped popularize this philosophy. Its migration from a monolithic Java app to a sprawling microservices ecosystem became the canonical case study taught at conferences and in blog posts worldwide.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But here’s the twist: buried in the operational reality of running one of the most complex streaming platforms on Earth, Netflix accidentally revealed a counterintuitive truth — under certain conditions, monoliths actually scale better.&#8221; <a href="mailto:https://medium.com/@yashbatra11111/how-netflix-accidentally-proved-monoliths-scale-better-than-microservices-f2d66f0a0bb5">Link</a></p>
