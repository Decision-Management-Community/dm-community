A remembrance of Marvin Minsky, the MIT cognitive scientist and AI co-founder whose research helped lay
the groundwork for both the personal computer and the modern AI field. Minsky's work on how machines
might represent knowledge and reason about the world remains foundational reading for anyone working
in decision automation.
