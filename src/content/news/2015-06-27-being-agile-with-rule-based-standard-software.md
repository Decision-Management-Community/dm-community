<p>Silvie Spreeuwenberg, a known authority in business rules, published a LinkedIn <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/being-agile-rule-based-standard-software-silvie-spreeuwenberg?trk=hb_ntf_MEGAPHONE_ARTICLE_POST">article</a> that puts in question a need for general purpose BRMS and gives the preference to business rules incorporated in specialized software products such as SalesForce.  <!--more-->She wrote:</p>
<blockquote><p>“My hypothesis is that business rules will <strong>not</strong> create a breakthrough in making organizations more flexible and agile”</p></blockquote>
<p>and made several more slightly controversial statements.  Silvie even wrote that after this article “s<em>ome say that I am an apostate or deserter of the business rules community</em>”.</p>
<p>Silvie still sees “a future for business rules” but probably not in the coming decade. What is your opinion?</p>
