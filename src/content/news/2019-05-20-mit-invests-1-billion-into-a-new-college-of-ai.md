<p>One of the birthplaces of artificial intelligence, MIT, <span class="jsx-2473920458 storyLink"><a class="" href="http://news.mit.edu/2018/mit-reshapes-itself-stephen-schwarzman-college-of-computing-1015" target="_blank" rel="noopener">has announced</a></span> a bold plan to reshape its academic program around the technology. With $1 billion in funding, MIT will create a new college that combines AI, machine learning, and data science with other academic disciplines. It is the largest financial investment in AI by any US academic institution to date. <a href="https://www.technologyreview.com/f/612293/mit-has-just-announced-a-1-billion-plan-to-create-a-new-college-for-ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
