<p>These days you may have heard stories of people <a href="http://www.vox.com/2016/7/11/12129162/pokemon-go-android-ios-game" target="_blank">hunting down Pokémon</a> on their office desks, in hospital rooms, and even in bathrooms. Read about <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2016/07/11/the-brilliant-mechanics-of-pokemon-go/" target="_blank">the brilliant mechanics of Pokémon Go</a>. Do you want to catch them all? As quickly as possible? There is a math model for that called the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travelling_salesman_problem" target="_blank">Traveling Salesman Problem</a> (TSP). There are many good TSP solvers that may check every possible route to pick out the shortest one. Read <a href="http://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/tsp/poke/" target="_blank">more</a></p>
