<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A few days ago at <a href="https://decisioncamp2024.wordpress.com/program/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">DecisionCAMP-2024</a>  Peter Voss presented &#8220;<a href="https://youtu.be/SrdtkJRVPIc" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><em>The Third Wave of AI: From rules, to statistics, to cognition</em></a>&#8221; explaining why LLMs are not on the path towards Cognitive AI [AGI]. Today Peter published even a stronger article titled &#8220;<em><a href="https://petervoss.substack.com/p/the-insanity-of-huge-language-models" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">The Insanity of Huge Language Models</a></em>&#8221; with supporting quotes from Yann LeCun, Demis Hassabis, and even Sam Altman. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Blending LLMs with Decision Intelligence(DI) Platforms was a hot topic at <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SwfV0gkuhxo" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">DecisionCAMP</a> and many vendors started adding AI Assistants to their DI products. At the same time the latest OpenAI o1-preview claims reasoning capabilities with which &#8220;<em>we&#8217;ll no longer need Symbolic AI to make good decisions</em>&#8220;. Will such claims be actually justified within real-world decision-making applications? With conflicting experiences and predictions our Community continues to apply Decision Intelligence with rule engines, machine learning, and optimization tools to build practical decisioning systems while cautiously experimenting with LLM-based assistants. Listen the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4spCC9Bh7c" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Closing Remarks</a> and share your expectations from DI platforms for 2025-26.</p>
