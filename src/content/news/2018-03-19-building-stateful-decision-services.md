<p>Sometimes a decision service needs to consider information or context from previous invocations of the service. For example, you might want to award a customer discount if they purchased more than two items in one week. The information you keep about each invocation is called the <em>state</em>, and a decision service that uses state in its business logic is <em>stateful</em>. Nigel Crowther, our presenter at DecisionCAMP-2017, wrote an <a href="https://developer.ibm.com/odm/2018/03/15/building-stateful-decision-services-odm-cloud/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">article</a> that discusses three ways you can build stateful decision services with IBM ODM.</p>
