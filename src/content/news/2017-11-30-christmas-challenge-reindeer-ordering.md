<p>Santa always leaves plans for his elves to determine the order in which the reindeer will pull his sleigh. This year, for the European leg of his journey, his elves are working to a schedule, which will form a single line of nine reindeer. There are 15 <a href="http://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-dec-2017/">reindeer ordering rules</a>. Our Challenge Dec-2017 asks you to help elves to determine the order of the reindeer.</p>
