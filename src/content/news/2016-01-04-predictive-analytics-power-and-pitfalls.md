<p>In the last 5 years predictive analytics demonstrated its power and experts <a href="/news/2015-12-08-5-predictions-for-2016-the-year-of-prescriptive-analytics/">predict</a> that 2016 will be a year of Business Analytics. However, those who use the proper tools for years are well aware of pitfalls of these techniques. While experts usually point to the complexity of data handling, there are several other important points listed in the following sources:<!--more--></p>
<ul>
<li><a href="http://datamininglab.com/it-is-a-mistake-to-accept-leaks-from-the-future" target="_blank">Leaks from the Future</a>: it describes how easy it is to accept leaks from the future in your modeling results and how important to scrutinize any input variable that works too well</li>
<li><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/automatically-learned-business-rules-should-we-them-jacob-feldman" target="_blank">Incomprehensibility of Automatically Discovered Rules</a>: business rules discovered by a predictive analytics model better to be comprehensible by business analysts.</li>
