<p>Real-world decision models frequently deal with collections of business objects. Some of our previous challenges and their solutions already demonstrated several use cases. The challenge &#8220;<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/make-a-good-burger/">Make a Good Burger</a>&#8221; even includes a <a href="/news-media/2015/06/garyhallmarkburger.pdf" target="_blank">DMN FEEL solution</a> that for the first time shows the use of FEEL&#8217;s loops and sorting facilities. Another related challenge is &#8220;<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-august-2015/" target="_blank">Define Duplicates</a>&#8221; that includes an <a href="/news-media/2015/08/rh-challenge-08-2015.pdf" target="_blank">R-based solution</a>.</p>
<p>A new, just published Sep-2015 Challenge &#8220;<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-sep-2015/" target="_blank">Collections of Cars</a>&#8221; specifies even more complex operations on collections of business objects (in this case &#8220;cars&#8221;). We expect to see more solutions that demonstrate capabilities of vendors who claim to be <a href="http://openjvm.jvmhost.net/DMNtools/" target="_blank">DMN-compliant</a> and not only from Corticon and OpenRules.</p>
