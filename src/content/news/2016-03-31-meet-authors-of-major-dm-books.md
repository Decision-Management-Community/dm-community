<li><strong>Alan Fish</strong>, <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Knowledge-Automation-Implement-Management-Processes/dp/111809476X/ref=pd_bxgy_b_img_y" target="_blank">Knowledge Automation: How to Implement Decision Management in Business Processes</a>, 2012</li>
</ul>
