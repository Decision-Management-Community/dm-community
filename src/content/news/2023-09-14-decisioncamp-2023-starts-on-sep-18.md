See the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/program/" target="_blank">Program</a> with the presentation slides and if you still haven&#8217;t registered, do it now for free at <a href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/registration/" rel="nofollow">https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/registration/</a>. </p>
