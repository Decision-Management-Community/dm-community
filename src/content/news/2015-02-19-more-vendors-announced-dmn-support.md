<p>Recently more vendors such as <a href="http://www2.sparklinglogic.com/product/smarts-pencil/" target="_blank">Sparkling Logic</a> and <a href="http://www.signavio.com/news/signavio-process-editor-version-8-4-0-saas/" target="_blank">Signavio</a> announced that their tools now support the DMN standard. They joined the initial DMN implementations provided by <a href="http://openrules.com" target="_blank">OpenRules</a>, <a href="http://www.sapiensdecision.com/" target="_blank">Sapiens</a>, and a few others. Nick Broom, a well-known DM practitioner and a contributor to the DMCommunity, provided his <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/redirect?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvisionalysis%2Eco%2Euk%2F2015%2F02%2F17%2Fcometh-the-hour-cometh-signavio%2F&amp;urlhash=_xp8&amp;_t=tracking_disc" target="_blank">review</a> of the Signavio implementation, and promised to provide a review of the FICO DMN Modeler that according to Alan Fish is coming next month &#8211; see this LinkedIn <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/groups/Another-DMN-tool-this-time-4225568.S.5950536769064697856?view=&amp;item=5950536769064697856&amp;type=member&amp;gid=4225568&amp;trk=eml-b2_anet_digest-hero-1-hero-disc-disc-0&amp;midToken=AQFHYWW_SEhf5w&amp;fromEmail=fromEmail&amp;ut=1bM_LPK0H5f6E1" target="_blank">post</a>.</p>
<p>We plan to prepare a complete list of the current BR&amp;DM vendors who support the DMN standard. This list along with reviews from practitioners will be published and maintained at this website. If you are a vendor and want to be included into this list, please update your profile at the <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/tools/" target="_blank">Live Catalogs of BR and DM tools</a> and <a href="mailto:DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com">email us</a> a link to your implementation.</p>
