<p>Equity and fairness have been studied from different point of views in philosophy, political science and economics, game theory, optimization, telecommunications, so pretty much everyone can find something to chew on. The <a href="http://sbebo.github.io/blog/blog/2015/11/21/fairness/" target="_blank">following blog-post</a> is about three weary wanderers who reached a pub in the middle of nowhere with only one liter of beer left. It shows how the definition of fairness (optimization objective) may dramatically affect various decisions to be made.</p>
