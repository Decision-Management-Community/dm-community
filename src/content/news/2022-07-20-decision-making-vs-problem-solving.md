<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This post <a href="https://www.sweetprocess.com/decision-making/">Decision-Making: How to Determine the Best Possible Choices</a> defines <strong>decision-making</strong> as &#8220;<em>the process by which people come to actionable decisions after a careful consideration of the facts and values of the situation and parties involved. Decision-making could either be rational or irrational, and every decision-making process results in a final choice, which would either prompt an action, or not</em>&#8220;. Contrary, <strong>problem solving</strong> &#8220;<em>is using creativity and discovery to determine all possible courses of action after carefully examining the information provided. Problem solving is considered by many as a step toward decision-making, hence, the information gathered and solutions provided are used in decision-making.</em>&#8221; <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.sweetprocess.com/decision-making/" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
