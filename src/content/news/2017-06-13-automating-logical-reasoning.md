This <em><a href="http://coherentknowledge.com/ai-that-mimics-human-reasoning/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">paper</a> claims game-changing advances</em> that have come in the form of a knowledge representation and reasoning language called <a href="http://benjamingrosof.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/aaai17-rulelog-tutorial-slides-final.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Rulelog</a>. Attendees of <a href="http://2017.ruleml-rr.org/program/#tutorials" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RuleML+RR</a> and <a href="http://2017.ruleml-rr.org/decisioncamp-2017-schedule/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DecisionCAMP</a>  will be able to listen its <a href="http://benjamingrosof.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/aaai17-rulelog-tutorial-slides-final.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">presentation</a> by Coherent Knowledge in London next month.</p>
