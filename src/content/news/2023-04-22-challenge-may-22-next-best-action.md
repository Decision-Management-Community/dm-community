<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“Next best action” is a popular decision-making strategy.&nbsp;But how to define the “best” next action? This challenge may demonstrate it. Consider an NxN&nbsp;grid of lightbulbs. We are given an initial state where some of the bulbs are&nbsp;<strong>off</strong>&nbsp;and some are&nbsp;<strong>on</strong>. Then, at every step you need to choose a bulb in the&nbsp;<strong>off</strong>&nbsp;state. It will turned on, and every other bulb in the row&nbsp;<strong>and</strong>&nbsp;in the column of the bulb will be toggled: If it was&nbsp;<strong>on</strong>, it turns&nbsp;<strong>off</strong>, and vice versa. The goal is to reach a grid where all the light bulbs are&nbsp;<strong>on</strong>. <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-may-2023/">Link</a></p>
