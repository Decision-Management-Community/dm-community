<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dec-2024 Challenge deals with an order pricing policy that depends on a number of different units, product types (Standard or Premium), country of origin,  etc. A seller uses a mixed bracket volume and cost policy for orders with multiple units:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>1-99 units:</strong> $10 per unit (Standard), $15 per unit (Premium)</li>



<li><strong>100-499 units:</strong> $9 per unit (Standard), $14 per unit (Premium)</li>



<li><strong>500+ units:</strong> $8 per unit (Standard), $13 per unit (Premium)</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For products that originated not in the US or Canada, there is a handling fee of $50 per order. If the order includes hazardous material, the total price should be increased by 30%. <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-dec-2024/">Link</a></p>
