Berk Orbay's essay "An Ode to Operations Research" made the case that OR — the discipline behind
optimization and constraint solving — deserves to be considered "AI" on the same footing as machine
learning, since both are fundamentally about supporting better decisions. It's a framing this community
has pushed for years, well before "decision intelligence" became a common label for it.
