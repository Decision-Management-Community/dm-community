<p>This month you need to build a decision model that is capable to decide which product lines inside sales orders are duplicate. The duplicate product lines can be recognized by user-defined similarity rules &#8211; read more <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-august-2015/">here</a>. In general, this challenge is supposed to demonstrate how different business rules and DMN tools deal with collections of objects.</p>
