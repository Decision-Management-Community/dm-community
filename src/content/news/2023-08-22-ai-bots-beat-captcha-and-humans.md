<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Now, a <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.12108.pdf" target="_blank">research paper (pdf)</a> published last month that has yet to be peer reviewed indicates that AI-automated attacks on various CAPTCHA schemes have been successful. The study, conducted by a group of researchers, showed that AI bots are now <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.newscientist.com/article/2384228-bots-are-better-at-beating-are-you-a-robot-tests-than-humans-are/" target="_blank">better than humans</a> at decoding the CAPTCHAs. They even create an impression of humans being more robots than the bots that the CAPTCHAs try to keep out. And they even do it much faster. <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://qz.com/ai-bots-recaptcha-turing-test-websites-authenticity-1850734350" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
