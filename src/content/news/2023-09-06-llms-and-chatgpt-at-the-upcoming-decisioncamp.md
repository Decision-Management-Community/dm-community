<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Large Language Models (LLMs) and ChatGPT will be well-presented at the upcoming <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/program" target="_blank">DecisionCAMP-2023</a> on Sep 18-20. Here are a few examples:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><a href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/program/#GaryHallmark" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>No Code Decision Model using ChatGPT&nbsp;</strong>by Gary Hallmark (Oracle)</a></li>



<li><a href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/program/#DenisGagne" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Low Code Neuro-Symbolic Agents: Prompt Engineering through Process and Decision Orchestration&nbsp;</strong>by Denis Gagne (Trisotech)</a></li>



<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/program/#JanVanthienen" target="_blank"><strong>GPT-3 for Decision Requirements Modeling and Advice&nbsp;</strong>by Jan Vanthienen and Alexandre Goossens (KU Leuven)</a></li>



<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://decisioncamp2023.wordpress.com/program/#DenisTom" target="_blank"><strong>Generative AI and Regulatory Compliance</strong></a> by Tom DeBevoise<strong> </strong>(Advanced Component Research) and Denis Gagne (Trisotech)</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The very first presentation by Gary Hallmark will demonstrate the use of ChatGPT to generate all of the decision logic in the well-known DMN Loan Approval Strategy decision using simple English prompts and natural language decision tables. </p>
