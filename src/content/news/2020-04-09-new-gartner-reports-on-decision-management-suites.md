<p><a href="http://www.gartner.com/document/3983270?sre_77063979" target="_blank" rel="noopener">How to Choose Your Best-Fit Decision Management Suite Vendor</a>, G00439904. Decision management suites go beyond business rule management systems by providing more features for designing, deploying, maintaining and auditing decision-making software. This report describes the steps data and analytics leaders should take to identify the best vendor for their business needs.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.gartner.com/document/3983267?sre_77063979" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Toolkit: Decision Management Suite Vendor Profiles</a><strong>, </strong>G00450843. Decision Management Suites vary in their business rule processing, analytics support, ease of development, operational features and geographic and industry support. This Toolkit will help data and analytics leaders create a shortlist of DMS vendors based on technical and business considerations.</p>
