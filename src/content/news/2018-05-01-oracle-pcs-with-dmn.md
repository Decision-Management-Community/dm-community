A combination of process and decision modeling simplifies business processes by eliminating and replacing sections of a process model with a decision model. The decision logic of the process model is captured as a separate, yet linked, model. A new version of Feb-2018 includes a DMN graphical modeler and even <a href="https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/paas/process-cloud/user/importing-and-exporting-decision-models.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">import/export</a> for DMN XML. <a href="http://blog.rubiconred.com/working-with-decision-model-and-notation-in-oracle-process-cloud-service/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link </a> <a href="https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/paas/process-cloud/user/creating-decision-model.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
