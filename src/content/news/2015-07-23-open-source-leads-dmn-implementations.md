<p class="post-details">It is only natural that open source vendors are among  the first companies who provide the implementations of the Decision Model &amp; Notation (DMN) standard. When the <a href="http://www.omg.org/spec/DMN/1.0/Beta2/" target="_blank">DMN 1.0</a> became publicly available for the very first time on September 26, 2013, it was an open source vendor, OpenRules, who just in a few weeks announced its first implementation of the <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/grp/post/4225568-5795340229346873344?goback=%2Egmr_4225568" target="_blank">DMN 1.0 Primer</a>. And today, on July 23, 2015, a <a href="http://www.linkedin.com/redir/redirect?url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww%2Esignavio%2Ecom%2Fnews%2Findustry-consortium-launches-dmn-open-source-project-onedecision-io%2F&amp;urlhash=BCAi&amp;_t=tracking_anet" target="_blank">new open industry initiative</a> was formed to promote the DMN standard &#8220;<em>by providing an <strong>open source reference implementation</strong> for automatic execution of DMN models.</em>&#8221; <!--more-->It currently includes: Signavio, Alfresco, Omny Link and Bruce Silver Associates &#8211; read the proper announcement <a href="http://www.linkedin.com/redir/redirect?url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww%2Esignavio%2Ecom%2Fnews%2Findustry-consortium-launches-dmn-open-source-project-onedecision-io%2F&amp;urlhash=BCAi&amp;_t=tracking_anet" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>
<p class="post-details">The complete current Catalog of DMN supporting tools can be found at <a href="http://openjvm.jvmhost.net/DMNtools" target="_blank">http://openjvm.jvmhost.net/DMNtools</a>/.  You even may compare their DMN implementations feature-by-feature. If your product also supports (or plans to support) the DMN, you may add it to the Catalog from <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/tools/adding-your-product-to-catalogs/" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>
