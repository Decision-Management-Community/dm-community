A look at an interview with Pegasystems founder and CEO Alan Trefler, given at a point when Pega's stock
had more than doubled over the previous year. The conversation covers how a company built in the 1980s
kept adapting its low-code and decisioning platform through successive waves of enterprise software —
relevant reading for anyone wondering how legacy rules/BPM vendors reinvent themselves.
