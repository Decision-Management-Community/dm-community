<p class="wp-block-paragraph">&#8220;<em>Will it give Microsoft a chance to overtake Google in the search space?</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It was only recently that&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2023/01/23/microsoftandopenaiextendpartnership" target="_blank">Microsoft announced</a>&nbsp;it would invest US$10 billion in OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, on top of a previous investment of a billion or more in 2022.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">ChatGPT has already been added to Microsoft’s Teams software. You can expect it to turn up soon in Word, where it will write paragraphs for you. In Outlook it will compose entire emails, and in PowerPoint it will help you prepare slides for your next talk.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Not to be outdone, Chinese web giant Baidu has also&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.theregister.com/2023/02/07/baidu_erniebot_generative_ai_chatbot/" target="_blank">sprung into action</a>. It&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/chinas-baidu-launch-chatgpt-style-bot-march-source-2023-01-30/" target="_blank">recently announced</a>&nbsp;its latest chatbot would be released in March. Baidu’s chatbot is will be trained on 50% more parameters than ChatGPT, and will be bilingual. The company’s share price jumped 15% in response.&#8221;</p>
