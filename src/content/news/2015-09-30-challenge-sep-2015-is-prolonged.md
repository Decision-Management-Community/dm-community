<p>We prolonged the Sep-2015 Challenge &#8220;<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-sep-2015/">Collections of Cars</a>&#8221; until October 31. We consider this challenge to be quite important from the real-world decision modeling perspective. We already received <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-sep-2015/#Solutions">5 solutions</a> that allow DM practitioners to see how to handle collections of objects in various decision management environments. For the first time we see the proper examples in DMN FEEL. Unfortunately such popular tools as IBM ODM, FICO and Drools haven&#8217;t provided their solutions yet, and we encourage these vendors to demonstrate the power of their tools to our readers.</p>
