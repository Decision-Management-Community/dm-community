<p>Real digital transformation is bigger than bots. First, it requires a <a href="https://www1.pega.com/technology/design-thinking-approach" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener"><u>design thinking</u></a> approach to identify desired outcomes, and then a holistic look at sales, service, AI/<strong>decisioning</strong>, and robotics as an integrated, end-to-end workflow.</p>
<p>The fastest way to earn ROI is to automate repetitive task work with attended desktop bots and use unattended RPA for the last mile of integration, automating integrations to apps, data, and legacy systems.</p>
