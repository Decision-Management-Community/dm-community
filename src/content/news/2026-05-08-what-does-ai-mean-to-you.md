<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This question was asked by John Brandon Elam. Here is Adam DeJans Jr&#8217;s answer: &#8220;<em>AI means the cost of clerical intelligence is going to zero. The email, the report, the dashboard commentary, the SQL query, the first draft of code, the spreadsheet explanation, etc… all of that is becoming disposable. The hard part was never producing words or predictions. <strong>The hard part is deciding what should happen next, under constraints, uncertainty, incentives, and dollars at risk</strong>. AI is useful only when it is chained to that economic reality. Otherwise, it is just a very expensive way to manufacture confidence.</em>&#8221; <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/johnbelam_what-does-ai-mean-to-you-share-7458268821322354688-X3-k?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=member_desktop&amp;rcm=ACoAAAAFIMoBtgF6zELsOiwFm0hGYH1dIteE6pQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
