<p>Alan Trefler, CEO at Pegasystems, just introduced &#8220;<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/new-way-operating-one-microjourney-time-alan-trefler/?trackingId=YaFcMJ0%2FQQes6%2FIkRETRfQ%3D%3D" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The new way of operating: One microjourney™ at a time</a>&#8220;: <em>What we’re really talking about are <strong>microjourneys</strong> – an optimized outcome for each customer touchpoint that, for example, changes the customer experience, builds a relationship, or achieves a specific organizational milestone. Acquiring a new customer, opening a new account, fulfilling an order, or resolving a billing inquiry all fit the definition of a microjourney. Applying a microjourney view to systems and operations can help businesses streamline both. </em>Pushing &#8220;microjourneys vs traditional business architectures&#8221; sounds very similar to the modern &#8220;microservices vs. monolithic technical architectures&#8221; approach. <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/new-way-operating-one-microjourney-time-alan-trefler/?trackingId=YaFcMJ0%2FQQes6%2FIkRETRfQ%3D%3D" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
