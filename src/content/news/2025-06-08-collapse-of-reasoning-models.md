<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Apple’s ML scientists put to the test the latest &#8220;reasoning&#8221; models, like Claude, DeepSeek-R1, and o3-mini. They made these models solve classic puzzles: Tower of Hanoi, Checker Jumping, River Crossing, and Blocks World. Their findings &#8220;<em>reveal fundamental limitations in current models: despite sophisticated self-reflection mechanisms, these models fail to develop generalizable reasoning capabilities beyond certain complexity thresholds</em>.&#8221; This puts in question the reasoning capabilities of these systems, showing that instead of &#8220;reasoning,&#8221; they just really well memorize patterns. Check out Apple&#8217;s original research paper, &#8220;<a href="https://ml-site.cdn-apple.com/papers/the-illusion-of-thinking.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">The Illusion of Thinking</a>&#8220;.</p>
