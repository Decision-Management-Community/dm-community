<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Brooks" target="_blank">Fred Brooks</a>, one of the most outstanding computer scientists of our time, died on Nov. 17. He authored &#8220;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mythical_Man-Month" target="_blank">The Mythical Man Month</a>&#8220;, a book that was hugely influential on so many of our readers. His paper &#8220;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Silver_Bullet" target="_blank">No Silver Bullet</a>&#8221; would be on most people&#8217;s shortlist of most influential papers.</p>
