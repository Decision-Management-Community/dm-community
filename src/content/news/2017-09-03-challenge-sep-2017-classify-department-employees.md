<p>Our <a href="http://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-sep-2017/">Challenge Sep-2017</a> asks you to help a human resource office to create a decision model that for each department calculates minimal, maximal, and average salaries along with a number of high-paid employees using rules like “Salary &gt; 85000”. Use any Business Rules and/or a DMN tool and submit your solution to <a href="mailto:DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com">DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com</a>.</p>
