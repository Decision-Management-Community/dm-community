---
title: "Is Operation Research walking into commoditization?"
date: 2026-10-30
tags: ["Operations Research", "Decision Optimization", "Generative AI"]
---

At least two OR experts just pointed in this direction.

Pierre Schaus wrote [https://lnkd.in/p/eGnCjv8R](https://lnkd.in/p/eGnCjv8R):

> “Coding agents can now formulate combinatorial optimization models from scratch, hack open-source solvers, and architect custom solving strategies in minutes.
>
> IMHO OR is walking into commoditization as data science did a bit before.
>
> If an AI can prototype an entire MIP or CP formulation and push open-source tools to their absolute limit, what will happen to commercial solver vendors?”

Meinolf Sellmann wrote [https://lnkd.in/p/ex9diSAv](https://lnkd.in/p/ex9diSAv):

> “I have stated this here before and will do so again: the job of an OR expert who predominantly develops models to be sent to a solver is going extinct. Two out of three respondents already use GenAI to produce code and to build models.”
