<p>As all votes were anonymous, it&#8217;s hard to tell how objective these results are: we do not know if voters selected certain decision models over others by actually comparing the models or by &#8220;voting for friends&#8221; with no comments. In 2018 we plan to select a small group of well-known DM experts, and ask them to compare all solutions for 2018 challenges and explain their reasons for selecting the winners.</p>
