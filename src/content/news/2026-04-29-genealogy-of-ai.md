Martin Milani's essay pushes back on the flood of AI infographics stacking architecture on top of
architecture and "term salad through a blender," arguing that most of them skip the one fundamental
question worth starting from: what is this system actually descended from, conceptually. A short,
opinionated read for anyone tired of buzzword-diagram AI explainers.
