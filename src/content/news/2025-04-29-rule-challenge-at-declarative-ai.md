<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This year <strong><a href="https://2025.declarativeai.net/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">DeclarativeAI</a> </strong> (that includes <a href="http://decisioncamp.org" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">DecisionCAMP</a> as a co-event) will run the <strong><a href="https://2025.declarativeai.net/events/ruleml-rr/rule-challenge" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">19th International Rule Challenge</a></strong>, fostering friendly competition among innovative rule-oriented tools, prototypes, and applications tailored to research, industry, and government. Participants are invited to showcase their solutions to self-defined challenges and to propose open challenges for the community to address. <a href="https://2025.declarativeai.net/events/ruleml-rr/rule-challenge" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></p>
