<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://blog.selman.org/2024/06/22/text-to-knowledge-graph/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Dan Selman</a> published an article that describes how to convert natural language text to knowledge graphs. Dan extended <a href="https://github.com/accordproject/lab-concerto-graph" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Concerto Graph</a> by adding a new method <code>mergeTextToGraph</code> which simplifies the conversion of a block of text to a Knowledge Graph ensuring that the structure of the nodes and edges conforms to the Concerto data model that is associated with the Graph Model. All source codes are available and you may try to test it with your own data models. <a href="https://blog.selman.org/2024/06/22/text-to-knowledge-graph/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></p>
