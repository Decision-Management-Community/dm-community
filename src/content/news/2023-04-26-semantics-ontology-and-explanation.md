<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While ChatGPT dominates social forums, scientists quietly continue to work on real understanding of our surrounding using 100-years-old concepts of <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.amazon.com/Logic-Semantics-Metamathematics-Papers-1923/dp/0915144751" target="_blank">Logic, Semantics</a>, and more recently <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology_(computer_science)" target="_blank">Ontologies</a>. Computer science people build symbolic models to represent their assumptions about a certain domain using some kind of formal semantics in order to use, and especially in order to share these models. This <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.11124.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">paper</a> extends traditional conceptual models that need to be explained in terms of their ontological commitments to the world. This process of explanation is a process of revealing the real-world semantics of that model. <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.11124.pdf" target="_blank">Link</a> Consider this simple example: </p>
