<p>A new article <a href="http://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08813" target="_blank" rel="noopener">EU regulations on algorithmic decision-making and a &#8220;right to explanation&#8221;</a><br />
talks about the potential impact that the European Union&#8217;s new General Data Protection Regulation will have on the routine use of machine learning algorithms. Slated to take effect as law across the EU in 2018, the law will also create a &#8220;right to explanation,&#8221; whereby a user can ask for an explanation of an algorithmic decision that was made about them.</p>
