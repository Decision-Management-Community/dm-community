<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A team of computer scientists has come up with a&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.00671" target="_blank">dramatically faster algorithm</a>&nbsp;for one of the oldest problems in computer science: Maximum Flow. The problem asks how much material can flow through a network from a source to a destination if the links in the network have capacity limits. As an example, imagine a network of highways on which you’d like to send as many delivery trucks as possible from Los Angeles to New York City in a given amount of time.
