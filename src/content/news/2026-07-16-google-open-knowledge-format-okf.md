<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Google has introduced Open Knowledge Format (OKF), a new open specification designed to standardize the way content is packaged and shared with AI agents. <a href="https://x.com/GoogleCloudTech/status/2067012903337664886" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Cloud Tech’s X post </a>describes it as a &#8220;vendor-neutral&#8221; standard, built to be readable by both humans and machines without requiring new tools or software to implement. OKF formalizes the &#8220;LLM-wiki pattern&#8221; into a portable, interoperable format that provides a standardized way to represent enterprise knowledge for modern AI systems.
