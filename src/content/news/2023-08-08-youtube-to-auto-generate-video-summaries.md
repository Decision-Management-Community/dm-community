<p class="wp-block-paragraph" id="speakable-summary">YouTube is running a new test to auto-generate video summaries with the use of AI. As&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/18138167" target="_blank">noted</a>&nbsp;on the support page, the summaries have begun appearing on the watch and search pages, but are only available for a limited number of English-language videos and viewers.  Overall, it’s too early to tell how the AI summaries will affect YouTube creators and if it’ll actually help write their video summaries. But we’re curious to see how well the newest experiment performs and if it gets a wider rollout. <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/01/youtube-experiments-with-ai-auto-generated-video-summaries/" target="_blank">Link</a></p>
