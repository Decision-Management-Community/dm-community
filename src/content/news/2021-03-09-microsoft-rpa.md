<p>Microsoft announced Power Automate Desktop for Windows 10, which provides an attended RPA solution to automate tasks in SharePoint, Excel, Outlook, OneNote, and third-party business apps. Web and desktop recorders capture steps in an automation, and a visual drag-and-drop designer organizes flows logically, with over 370 prebuilt actions that connect to a number of different applications. <a href="https://venturebeat.com/2021/03/02/microsoft-brings-rpa-to-windows-10-with-new-power-platform-products/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
