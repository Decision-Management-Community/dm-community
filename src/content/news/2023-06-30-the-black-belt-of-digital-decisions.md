<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In Jan-2017 Mike Gualtieri from Forrester published a report &#8220;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.forrester.com/report/Prescriptive-Analytics-The-Black-Belt-Of-Digital-Decisions/RES122982" target="_blank">Prescriptive Analytics: The Black Belt Of Digital Decisions</a>&#8221; which tied Analytics to actionable Decisions. Mike wrote: &#8220;<em>Enterprises must stop wasting time and money on unactionable analytics. These efforts don’t matter if the resulting analytics don’t lead to better insights and <strong>decisions</strong> that are specifically linked to measurable business outcomes.</em>&#8221; Since then the term &#8220;prescriptive analytics&#8221; was replaced with &#8220;Decision Intelligence&#8221; but the listed technologies became even more important today:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Descriptive analytics</strong>: enables analytics users within an enterprise to query data integrated from multiple applications</li>



<li><strong>Predictive analytics</strong>: creates predictive models</li>



<li><strong>Streaming analytics</strong>: detects events and patterns in real-time streams of data</li>



<li><strong>Search and knowledge discovery</strong>: leads to insights, and insights lead to knowledge</li>



<li><strong>Simulation</strong>: imitates a real-world process or system over time using a computer model</li>



<li><strong>Mathematical optimization</strong>: the process of finding the optimal solution to a problem that has numerically expressed constraints</li>



<li><strong>Machine learning</strong>: identify patterns or make predictions by analyzing historical data that is representative of the domain</li>



<li><strong>Pragmatic AI: </strong>continuously learn from new information, build knowledge, and then use that knowledge to make decisions and interact with people and/or other machines. <a href="https://www.forrester.com/blogs/17-02-20-what_exactly_the_heck_are_prescriptive_analytics/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">LINK</a></li>
</ul>
