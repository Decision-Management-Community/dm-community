<p>Following the announcement from <a href="http://blog.athico.com/2016/10/drools-7-to-support-dmn-decision-model.html" target="_blank">last year</a>, the popular open source Drools rules engine added full run-time support for DMN at compliance level 3 into its upcoming version 7.0. As of January/2017, the implementation is feature complete and the team is working on additional nice to have capabilities and the eventual bug fixes in preparation for the release. See Drools/DMN <a href="http://blog.athico.com/2017/01/dmn-runtime-example-with-drools.html" target="_blank">solution </a>of our Oct-2016 Challenge &#8220;<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-oct-2016/">Flight Rebooking</a>&#8220;</p>
