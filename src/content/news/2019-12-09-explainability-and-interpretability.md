The recent paper &#8220;<a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.10154.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Stop explaining  black box machine learning models for high stakes decisions and use interpretable models instead&#8221;</a> shows the difference between explainability and interpretability, and states that the former may be problematic. <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.10154.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Link</a></p>
