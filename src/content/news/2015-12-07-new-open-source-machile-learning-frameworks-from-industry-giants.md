<p>Within one month we saw one IT giant after another coming up with “open-source” frameworks for ML / Advanced Analytics. This can’t be a coincidence! <!--more--></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>IBM</strong> most recently announced <a href="http://researcher.watson.ibm.com/researcher/view_group.php?id=3174" target="_blank">SystemML</a> – specializing on abstraction and model generation.</li>
<li><strong>Microsoft</strong> announced just 2 weeks ago: <a href="http://www.dmtk.io/" target="_blank">DMLT</a> – Distributed Machine Learning Toolkit</li>
<li><strong>Intel</strong> announced just before their <a href="http://trustedanalytics.org/" target="_blank">TAP</a> (Trusted Analytics Platform) – specializing on a secure data platform.</li>
<li><strong>Google</strong> did similar with <a href="https://tensorflow.org/" target="_blank">TensorFlow</a> – specializing on deep learning and flexible approaches to parallelization.</li>
</ul>
