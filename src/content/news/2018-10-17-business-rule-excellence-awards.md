The first annual Business Rules Excellence Awards (BREA), created by a group of business-rules
practitioners and vendors, recognized organizations that had demonstrably excelled at putting rules-based
and decision-management systems into production — a rare moment of the community publicly celebrating
real-world implementations rather than just tooling and standards.
