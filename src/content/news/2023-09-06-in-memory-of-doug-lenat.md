A remembrance of Douglas Lenat, who died at 72 after decades spent trying to give computers common
sense through the Cyc project ("Intelligence is ten million rules," as he put it). Lenat had spoken to
this community directly back in 2012, making his death a more personal loss than most AI-history
obituaries.
