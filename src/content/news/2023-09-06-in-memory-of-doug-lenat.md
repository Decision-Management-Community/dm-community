<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Lenat" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Douglas Lenat</a>, who Tried to Make A.I. More Human, died at 72. He spent decades working on artificial intelligence, striving to create computers that could replicate common sense. In 2012 Doug was our keynote speaker at <a href="/news-media/2016/01/intellifest2012-flyer.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IntelliFest</a>, a predecessor of DecisionCAMP. Here are two good articles about Doug: in <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2023/09/04/technology/douglas-lenat-dead.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NYTimes</a> and by <a href="https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/doug-lenat-1950-2023?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=android&amp;r=r7r4o" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Gary Marcus</a>. And here is a link to his last article <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.04445.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Getting from Generative AI to Trustworthy AI: What LLMs might learn from Cyc</a>.</p>
