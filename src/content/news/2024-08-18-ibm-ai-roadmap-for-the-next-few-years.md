<ul class="wp-block-list">

<li>2025: Alter the Scaling of Generative AI with Neural Architectures Beyond Transformers</li>



<li>2026: Bring Robust Strategic Reasoning and Commonsense Knowledge to AI</li>

<li>2030+: Build Adaptable and Generalist AI for Effective Human-Machine Collaboration</li>
