<p>Handling exceptions in your business logic is one of the most typical and non-trivial problem in decision management.  There are two new posts <a href="http://www2.sparklinglogic.com/dmblog/2015/11/17/override-rule/" target="_blank">Can you Override that Rule?</a> and <a href="http://www2.sparklinglogic.com/dmblog/2015/10/27/rules-everyone/" target="_blank">Are the Rules the Same for Everyone?</a> from Carole-Ann Matignon of Sparkling Logic that provide dos and don&#8217;ts for exception handling. You may also want to analyze different solutions for our Oct-2014 Challenge &#8220;<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-oct-2014/">Borrowing PASS/FAIL Decision with Mitigation Criteria</a>&#8220;.</p>
