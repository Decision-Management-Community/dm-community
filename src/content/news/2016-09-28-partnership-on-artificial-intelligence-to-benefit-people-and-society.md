<p>Today a new nonprofit organization called the <a href="http://www.partnershiponai.org/" target="_blank">Partnership on Artificial Intelligence to Benefit People and Society</a> announced its establishment. Participants include Amazon, Google (and Google’s DeepMind) Facebook, IBM, and Microsoft. Those companies will put up money and research resources (that could mean talent, open-source code, or data).
