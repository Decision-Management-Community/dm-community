<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyc">Cyc</a> was a &#8220;child&#8221; of Doug Lenat, who devoted 40 years to trying to make AI more human. David Reed just blogged about it: &#8220;<em>Is Cyc dead, as the <a href="https://yuxi-liu-wired.github.io/essays/posts/cyc/">obituary</a> here claims? Did Deep Neural Nets kill it? I think they both have been part of a process that is killing AI by focusing only on &#8216;Representation of knowledge&#8217;.</em> <em>My own view is that the neural net training approach used in all LLMs today is not the way to get &#8216;intelligence&#8217;. Nor is the pure Cyc-style</em> approach. Both focus only on manipulating Representation, and in particular representations amenable to simple computation machines (neural networks or symbolic logic). Intelligence,<em> such as what we see in humans, which I call Cognition, isn&#8217;t primarily contained within the skull.</em>&#8221; <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/activity-7316116857801834496-WtSS?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=member_desktop&amp;rcm=ACoAAAAFIMoBtgF6zELsOiwFm0hGYH1dIteE6pQ" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></p>
