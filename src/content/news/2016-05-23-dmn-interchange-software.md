<p>As a community, we would like to maintain different software pieces that can be shared and reused by vendors and practitioners to promote Decision Management in general and the DMN standard in particular. So, we added a special section &#8220;<a href="https://dmcommunity.org/resources/community-software/">Community Software</a>&#8221; to the menu &#8220;Resources&#8221;. The first piece called &#8220;<strong><a href="https://dmcommunity.org/resources/community-software/dmn-interchange-software/">DMN Interchange Software</a></strong>&#8221; with all sources has been kindly contributed by <a href="http://openrules.com" target="_blank">OpenRules</a> and is available for free downloads. Consider to expand this piece and to share your own DM related software.</p>
