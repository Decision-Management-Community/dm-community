<p><a href="http://dmcommunity.org/">DMCommunity.org</a> is organizing a special event &#8220;DMN at BBC&#8221; during the <a href="http://www.businessrulesforum.com/" target="_blank">Business Rules and Decisions Forum 2015</a> (BBC) on November 2-6, 2015 in the Caesars Palace, Las Vegas, NV. We encourage all our members, visitors, and DM practitioners to attend this major annual Decision Management event<!--more-->– use the discount code <b>DMCLV15 </b>to get a <strong>20% discount</strong> on the Conference Passports.</p>
<p>There will be many DMN related sessions at the BBC &#8211; see their descriptions <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/grp/post/4225568-6012096047269101570?trk=groups-post-b-title" target="_blank">here</a>. The event &#8220;<strong>DMN at BBC</strong>&#8221; will take place on <strong>November 5, 2015 at 5:50PM</strong> right after the presentation of Prof. Jan Vanthienen, a member of the DMN Task Force. It will be open for all BBC attendees. We position this event as an informal meeting with participation of DMN practitioners, vendors and representatives of DMN RTF, many of whom will attend the event. We plan to exchange opinions about the current and future states of the <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/standards/">DMN standard</a>.</p>
