<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge-apr-2026/">April-2026 Challenge</a> aims to explore how LLMs orchestrate rule-based decision services. It describes three loosely coupled medical services:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Determine Medication and Dosing</li>



<li>Determine Creatinine Clearance</li>



<li>Drug Interaction Rules</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your LLM should use these three services (instead of its own general &#8220;knowledge&#8221;) to determine a therapy for a patient with Acute Sinusitis. Here is a possible request: &#8220;<em>I have a patient diagnosed with Acute Sinusitis. He is 58 years old, weighs 78 kg, and has a creatinine level of 1.85. Keep in mind that he is Penicillin-allergic and takes Coumadin.</em>&#8221; <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge-apr-2026/">Link</a></p>
