However, the specification itself uses this case only to demonstrate possible implementations of DRDs, BKM, decision tables, and other DMN features but not the best decision modeling practice.<!--more--></p>
<p>This challenge is not a competition of tools. Today there are <a href="http://openjvm.jvmhost.net/DMNtools/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">many tools</a> that claim their support of the DMN standard. Submitters may use these or any other tool to demonstrate how to create loan origination decision models which they consider to be the best from business (!) decision modeling, execution, and further maintenance perspectives.  Everybody may submit already published decision models or new implementations to <a href="mailto:decisionmanagementcommunity@gmail.com">decisionmanagementcommunity@gmail.com</a>.</p>
<p>All solutions should be submitted during June-2017 to be available from this website by July 2, 2017, 10 days before the start of the <a href="http://2017.ruleml-rr.org/decisioncamp-2017/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DecisionCAMP</a>.</p>
<p>To name the best solutions we plan to do the following:</p>
<ul>
<li>Have an open discussion that compares different implementations at the <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/groups/4225568" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LinkedIn DMN Discussion Group</a></li>
<li>Vote for the best 3 solutions of the Loan Origination decision model during the <a href="http://2017.ruleml-rr.org/decisioncamp-2017/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DecisionCAMP</a> in London.</li>
</ul>
