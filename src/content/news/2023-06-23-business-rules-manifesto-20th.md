The Business Rules Manifesto turned 20 in 2023 — two pages, free, translated into 18 languages, and
still, per the community's assessment, "as fresh as the day it was written." It marked the start of a
distinct way of thinking about rules as first-class business assets rather than buried application
logic, and this anniversary post is a good excuse to point newcomers to the original text.
