Martin Milani argues this isn't an argument against AI, but against outsourcing the thing that made us
capable of building AI in the first place. Tools that extend human thinking are fine; the risk is in
quietly handing over reasoning and operational decisions to systems rather than keeping people
accountable for them — a position squarely in line with why this community exists.
