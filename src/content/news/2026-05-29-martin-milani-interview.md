<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/martinmilani/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Martin Milani</a>: &#8220;<em>This is not an argument against AI, it is an argument for not outsourcing the thing that made us capable of building it. It&#8217;s manageable when any of these tools simply extend human thinking and intelligence. It becomes a serious problem when we start delegating thinking, reasoning, judgment, sequencing, and operational decisions to systems optimized for linguistic continuation, not thinking or reasoning. The deeper issue isn&#8217;t about AI. It&#8217;s about what happens to us and the shift.</em>&#8220;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">&#8220;<em>Civilizations advance when humans reason, challenge assumptions, and look for causes beneath appearances. The real danger isn&#8217;t that AI sounds intelligent.
