<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This problem used to be one of the popular <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.infoworld.com/article/2677351/benchmarking-the-rules-engine.html" target="_blank">benchmarks</a> for rule engines 20 years ago. It is interesting to see how <em>modern </em>decision engines can represent and solve this problem today. So, Miss Manners is throwing a party, and being a good host, she wants to arrange good seating. She wants to seat the guests in a boy-girl-boy-girl arrangement so that each guest will have someone on the left or right that has a common hobby.&nbsp;Help Miss Manners to do it for parties with 16, 32, 64, and 128 guests described in the Excel file that you may download from <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://github.com/DMCommunity/dmcommunity_shared/blob/master/MissMannersData.xls" target="_blank">here</a>. Please <a href="mailto:DecisionManagementCommunity@gmail.com">submit</a> your solutions using your favorite decision modeling tool. <a href="https://dmcommunity.org/challenge/challenge-june-2023/">Link</a></p>
