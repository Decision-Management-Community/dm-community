<p>Prof. Bob Kowalski and his colleagues at Imperial College London announced the <a href="http://blog.ruleml.org/post/122149540-announcing-the-logical-contracts-server#disqus_thread" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Logical Contracts Server</a> that incorporates extensions of LPS (Logic Production System) to facilitate deployment of smart contracts. Its <a href="http://logicalcontracts.com/server/">website</a> contains a link to a preliminary version, now available for trial. It also contains a short, three minute <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?time_continue=1&amp;v=wXP_UrcqMpY" target="_blank" rel="noopener">video</a>, illustrating an application to the paper-rock-scissor game, with an interface to the Ethereum blockchain.</p>
