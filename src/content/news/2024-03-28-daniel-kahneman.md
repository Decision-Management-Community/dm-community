Daniel Kahneman, the psychologist whose Nobel Prize–winning work on cognitive bias reshaped how we think
about human decision-making, died in March 2024 at 90. His book Thinking, Fast and Slow remains one of
the most directly relevant outside texts for anyone doing decision-management work, and is worth
reading (or re-reading) in his memory.
