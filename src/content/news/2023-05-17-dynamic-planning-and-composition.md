<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://ai.googleblog.com/2023/05/using-reinforcement-learning-for.html" target="_blank">article</a> summarizes recent advances in dynamic planning for human-to-assistant conversations. Many types of conversations, from gathering information to offering recommendations, require a flexible approach and the ability to modify the original plan for the conversation based on its flow. This ability to shift gears in the middle of a conversation is known as <em>dynamic planning</em>, as opposed to <em>static planning</em>, which refers to a more fixed approach. Unlike LLM methods, this approach gives the assistant the ability to fully control the source, correctness, and quality of the content that it may offer. At the same time, it can achieve flexibility via a learned dialogue manager that selects and combines the most appropriate content. <a href="https://ai.googleblog.com/2023/05/using-reinforcement-learning-for.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Link</a></p>
